# Brief: Loanleaf partner SEO landing pages

You are writing ONE landing page for loanleaf.co, aimed at UK finance brokers and lenders who want to buy leads. Output is a single JSON file (format at the bottom). Read this whole brief first.

## Audience and goal
Readers are UK commercial finance brokers, specialist brokers and direct lenders deciding where to buy leads. The page must rank for its keyword cluster AND be genuinely useful: explain what these leads are, how Loanleaf qualifies and prices them, how to work them, and how to start. Every page ends with a clear prompt to sign up on Loanleaf Platform or apply to become a partner.

## Hard rules
- 1,600 to 2,300 words of body copy (sections + FAQs), not counting the meta fields.
- British English. Plain, calm, confident tone. Short paragraphs (2 to 4 sentences). No hype, no exclamation marks.
- NO em dashes (—) or en dashes used as dashes anywhere. Use commas, full stops, colons or brackets. Hyphens in words are fine. Use "to" for ranges (£20 to £300).
- Use the primary keyword in the H1, the summary, one H2 and naturally a few times in the body. Use each secondary keyword at most 2 to 3 times, only where it reads naturally. No stuffing, no keyword lists.
- Do NOT invent anything: no statistics, volumes, conversion rates, customer numbers, testimonials, lender names, awards, partner names, or "industry data". Do not claim to be "the best", "number one" or "the UK's leading". You may explain why Loanleaf's approach is strong using the facts below (exclusive, pre-qualified, priced by fundability, real time, transparent).
- Do not give financial advice or tell brokers how to comply with FCA rules beyond "you are responsible for your own regulatory position".
- Be honest about limits listed in your page's notes (e.g. what the form does NOT ask).
- Don't copy sentences from loanleaf.co/partners; write fresh copy.

**What each lead contains**
- Loan amount; purpose (Growth and expansion, Cash flow, Equipment or vehicles, Stock and inventory, Commercial property (buy, build or refurbish), Refinance existing debt, Tax or VAT bill, Something else); how soon they need it (As soon as possible, Within 2 weeks, Within a month, Just exploring options); company structure (Ltd or LLP); annual revenue band (£50k to £100k, £100k to £250k, £250k to £500k, £500k to £1m, £1m to £5m, £5m+); time trading band (6 to 12 months, 1 to 2 years, 2 to 5 years, 5+ years); missed payments, defaults or CCJs in the last 6 years (No, Yes, Not sure); existing finance (yes/no, with amount left to repay and monthly repayments bands).

**Exclusivity and buying**
- Each lead is sold to ONE broker only. Once bought it disappears for everyone else and is never resold.
- Leads are sold on Loanleaf Platform (platform.loanleaf.co), a self-serve portal. Brokers see a summary of every live lead before buying: amount, purpose, timeline, revenue, trading time, credit history, existing finance, quality score, and masked contact details (e.g. a***@b****.co.uk, +447*******90) with "valid UK mobile" and "domain verified" checks. Business name and full contact details unlock on purchase.

Auto-buy buys matching leads the moment they arrive; if several brokers match, a fair rotation decides. Leads above the per-lead maximum are left for manual buying and the broker gets an email alert.
- Manual-mode brokers can get an email when a matching lead arrives.
- Bought leads are delivered instantly: on the platform, by email, and to the broker's CRM by webhook (JSON), which works with Zapier, Make, HubSpot, Pipedrive and any CRM that accepts webhooks.
- The platform includes a simple pipeline (New, Contacted, Application, Funded, Lost) with notes, and CSV export.

**Pricing**
- 1 credit = £1 of lead value. Each lead costs £20 to £300 in credits, set by a scoring model: loan size, revenue, time trading, credit history and affordability (whether the amount asked for is realistic for the business's revenue). A sensible ask from an established business costs more; a long shot costs the minimum. Examples from the live model: £25k loan, £100k to £250k revenue, 1 to 2 years trading = 40 credits; £100k loan, £250k to £500k revenue, 2 to 5 years = 95; £250k loan, £1m to £5m revenue, 5+ years = 165; £1m loan, £1m to £5m revenue, 2 to 5 years = 205; £5m ask from a 6 to 12 month old business with £100k to £250k revenue = 15.

- Unused credits roll over while subscribed and expire when the subscription ends.
- Refunds: within 72 hours of purchase, only if the phone number is not in service or belongs to someone else, or the email address does not exist. No refunds for any other reason.

**Getting started**

- Or apply at /partners/apply to talk to the team first.
- Regulatory: brokers are responsible for their own regulatory position, including FCA authorisation where their activities require it. Leads are limited companies and LLPs, which keeps them focused on commercial lending.
