What’s new on Loanleaf

Hi Jo,

A quick update on a few things we’ve changed this week. Most of them came straight from brokers telling us what would help.

Enquiries are up 21% on last week.

See if they own their home

Every opportunity now shows whether the director is a homeowner or a tenant, alongside revenue, time trading and credit. You see it before you buy, and you can set auto-buy to homeowners only.

Browse opportunities

Message them on WhatsApp

Each lead you buy now has a WhatsApp button next to Call and Email. It opens with a short first message already filled in, so you can edit it or just hit send.

On their side, the business gets a WhatsApp from us as soon as they’re matched with you, with your name and how to reach you, so they’re expecting to hear from you.

New and improved My leads

Search your leads, filter by purpose or revenue, and archive the ones you’re finished with. Export gives you a CSV of whatever you’re looking at, so one stage or one week is easy to pull into your CRM.

Open My leads

Every lead matched on Companies House

We only pass on limited companies and LLPs we can find on Companies House. You get the company details with every lead, including when it was set up, its industry and whether its filings are up to date.
