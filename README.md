# Leaflegal

Lead generation site for UK law firms, live at leaflegal.co (not yet pointed). Seeded from the LoanLeaf site
(`docs/LOANLEAF-OVERVIEW.md` describes how that works; `docs/LOANLEAF-CLONE-SPEC.md` lists every legal swap).

## Practice areas

Seven only: conveyancing, immigration, family law, debt recovery (business to business),
licensing, lease extensions and trademarks (with related IP such as copyright and design rights). Page copy lives in `tools/areas_content.py`.

- Immigration is referral only. The site never gives immigration advice (Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 s84).
- No personal injury, clinical negligence (LASPO s56 referral fee ban), housing disrepair or claims management.
- Conveyancing, family, licensing, lease extensions and debt recovery: England and Wales only.
- No motoring offences or other criminal matters (removed 22 Nov 2026). SRA Code 5.1(d) bars firms paying introducers for clients facing criminal proceedings.
- Trademarks are UK wide (one UK register). Firms are SRA solicitors or IPReg regulated chartered trade mark attorneys.

## How it works now

- Static pages are generated by Python: `sh tools/build_all.sh` (legal pages, site pages, then `tools/test_enquiry.mjs`).
- The client form is `public/assets/apply.js` and posts to `api/enquiry.js`, which emails the team (`ENQUIRY_TO`)
  and sends the person a confirmation without any matter details. A person then picks one regulated firm.
- Law firms apply at `/partners/apply` (`public/assets/partner-apply.js`, `api/partner.js`).
- There is no broker or firm platform yet. `public/platform`, `api/platform.js` and the Supabase schema are LoanLeaf's
  and are kept for later; `/platform` redirects home.

## Privacy rules

Matter details can be special category or criminal offence data (UK GDPR Art. 9 and 10).
They never go to Meta or LinkedIn. Tracking only runs with marketing consent, and the Meta pixel and LinkedIn tag IDs
in `public/assets/track.js` are empty until Leaflegal has its own.

## Environment variables

`RESEND_API_KEY`, `ENQUIRY_TO`, `ENQUIRY_FROM` (a verified leaflegal.co sender), and optionally `BIRD_API_KEY` (phone and email checks),
`TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` and `TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY`, `META_CAPI_TOKEN` and `META_PIXEL_ID`.
