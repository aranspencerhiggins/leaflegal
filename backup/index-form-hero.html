Questions

Is Loan Leaf a lender?
No. Loan Leaf is a free introduction service. We pass your enquiry to a specialist business finance broker who can look at the options for your business. We are not a lender or a broker, we don't give financial advice, and we are not authorised by the FCA.
Does it cost anything?
No. Our service is free to use. We may be paid by the broker we introduce you to. Any fees a broker or lender charges should be explained to you before you agree to anything.
Will filling in the form affect my credit score?
No. Our form doesn't involve a credit check. A broker or lender may run checks later on, and they should tell you before they do.
Who will contact me?
One independent finance broker we match you with, usually by phone, email or text. Brokers may or may not be authorised by the FCA, and you can check a firm on the FCA Register.
How much can my business borrow?
Brokers can help with funding from around £5,000 to over £2m. What's available depends on your business, its revenue and its credit history.
Can sole traders or start-ups apply?
Not at the moment. We can only help UK limited companies and LLPs that have been trading for at least 6 months, with annual revenue of £50,000 or more.
What happens to my information?
We only share your details with the broker we match you with, and only if you agree. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details.
