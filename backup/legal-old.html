FAQs
How does the monthly commitment work?
You agree to a rolling monthly commitment for a set number of leads, with a minimum purchase of 50 leads per month.
Are leads exclusive to our firm?
Yes. Leads are supplied exclusively to your firm unless otherwise specified in your contract.
What counts as a valid lead?
A valid lead includes accurate contact details and matches the agreed service scope. A lead remains valid even if the individual later decides not to proceed or is no longer interested.
Do all leads convert into clients?
No. As with any lead generation activity, not all leads will convert into clients. The service provides enquiries, not guaranteed instructions.
Which practice areas do you currently support?
We currently generate leads for insolvency and director legal advice, liquidation legal advice and immigration law.
Can you support other areas of law?
Yes. Additional practice areas can be supported on request.
Do you offer custom support for law firms?
Yes. Through our marketing agency, we can provide additional support across areas such as paid media, landing pages and broader digital marketing services.
Is there a long-term contract?
The service operates on a rolling monthly basis, subject to the agreed minimum lead volume.
