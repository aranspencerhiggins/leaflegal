Licensing in Reading

Pubs, bars, restaurants, late night venues and drivers deal with the Reading council’s licensing team. A licensing solicitor who knows how local committees work is a real advantage.

Leaflegal introduces people and businesses in Reading, Wokingham, Bracknell and across Berkshire to regulated law firms. Many firms work remotely as well as from their offices, so you can speak to a specialist by phone or video without travelling.

Licensing law covers the permissions businesses and individuals need to sell alcohol, provide late night refreshment, run gambling premises or drive a taxi. Applications, variations and reviews all have their own procedures and deadlines.
