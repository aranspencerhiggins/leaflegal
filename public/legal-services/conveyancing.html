About conveyancing

Conveyancing is the legal work involved in transferring a property from one owner to another. A conveyancer checks the title, carries out searches, raises questions with the other side, handles the money and registers the change of ownership with HM Land Registry.

Instead of ringing round for quotes, you answer a few questions once. We pass your enquiry to one firm regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority or the Council for Licensed Conveyancers, and they contact you with a quote and their timescales.

Things to know

Fixed fees and disbursements

Most firms quote a fixed legal fee plus disbursements such as searches, Land Registry fees and Stamp Duty Land Tax. Ask for a full breakdown so you can compare like for like.

Leasehold properties

Buying or selling a leasehold flat usually takes longer and costs more, because the firm also deals with the lease, the freeholder and the managing agent.

Your lender’s panel

If you are buying with a mortgage, the conveyancer usually needs to be on your lender’s panel. The firm can confirm this when they quote.

Conveyancing questions and answers

Does Leaflegal do the conveyancing?
No. Leaflegal is a free introduction service. The firm we introduce you to does the legal work and is responsible for it.
Which areas do you cover?
Conveyancing enquiries for properties in England and Wales. Scotland and Northern Ireland have separate systems and are not covered at the moment.
How much does conveyancing cost?
It depends on the property, its value, whether it is leasehold and the firm. The firm will give you a written quote before you instruct them.
Do I have to use the firm you introduce?
No. There is no obligation. You can compare their quote with others and choose whoever suits you.
