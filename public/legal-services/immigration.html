About immigration

In the UK, only people who are regulated can give immigration advice or provide immigration services. That includes solicitors regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority and advisers registered with the Immigration Advice Authority (formerly the OISC).

Leaflegal is not regulated to give immigration advice and does not do so. We ask a few simple questions so the right firm can get in touch, then pass your enquiry to one regulated firm. Everything about your options, your application and the rules comes from them.

Things to know

Check who is advising you

Anyone giving you immigration advice must be regulated. You can check a solicitor on the SRA register or an adviser on the Immigration Advice Authority register.

Fees

Immigration firms charge their own fees, and Home Office application fees are separate. The firm should explain both before you go ahead.

Free help

If you can’t afford to pay, GOV.UK lists organisations that may be able to help for free, including for asylum.

Immigration questions and answers

Can Leaflegal tell me if I qualify for a visa?
No. We are not allowed to give immigration advice and we don’t. Only a regulated adviser can tell you about your options. We introduce you to one.
Who will contact me?
One firm regulated to give immigration advice, such as a firm of solicitors regulated by the SRA or an adviser registered with the Immigration Advice Authority.
Does it cost anything?
Our introduction is free. The firm will explain its own fees before you agree to anything.
Can you help with asylum?
We don’t introduce asylum enquiries at the moment. GOV.UK can point you to free, specialist help.
