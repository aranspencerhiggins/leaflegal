2. What Leaflegal does

Leaflegal is a free introduction service for people and businesses looking for legal help. You tell us about your matter. If you agree, we pass your enquiry to one independent, regulated law firm we think may be able to help, and the firm contacts you.

Please be aware that Leaflegal:

  • is not a law firm and does not provide legal services;
  • does not give legal advice, and nothing on this website is legal advice;
  • does not give immigration advice or services of any kind;
  • is not regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA) or any other legal regulator; and
  • does not guarantee that a firm will contact you, take on your matter, or achieve any particular result.

3. Who can use our service

Our service is for individuals and businesses. By submitting an enquiry you confirm that:

  • you are at least 18 years old;
  • the enquiry is about your own matter, or you are authorised to make it on behalf of the business or person concerned; and
  • for conveyancing, family law, motoring offences, licensing and lease extensions, the matter is governed by the law of England and Wales.

Debt recovery enquiries are for businesses recovering money owed by other businesses only. We do not deal with personal injury, clinical negligence, housing disrepair or financial mis-selling claims, or with matters where you are looking only for legal aid.

4. The law firms we introduce you to

  • Firms are independent businesses. They are not part of Leaflegal, and we do not act for them or for you.
  • Every firm we work with is regulated by the SRA, CILEx Regulation or the Council for Licensed Conveyancers. Immigration enquiries go only to firms or advisers allowed to give immigration advice under the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999.
  • Any legal service a firm provides is between you and the firm, on its own terms of business and client care letter. Please read them carefully.
  • The firm decides whether it can help you and what it charges. It should explain its fees before you agree to anything.
  • If you have a complaint about a firm, you should raise it with the firm first. If you are not happy with its answer, you can usually go to the Legal Ombudsman. Concerns about a solicitor's conduct can be reported to the SRA.
  • We are not responsible for what firms say or do, or for any legal service or advice they provide.

5. Your information

  • You must make sure the information you give us is true, accurate and complete. Firms rely on it.
  • You must only give information about other people if you are entitled to share it.
  • We will only share your enquiry with a firm if you tick the box agreeing to it. The firm may then contact you by phone, email or text message about your enquiry.
  • We use your information as described in our Privacy Policy.

We may decide not to pass on an enquiry, for example if it is outside the areas we cover or appears to be false.

6. Cost and how we are paid

Our service is free for you to use. The firm we introduce you to pays us a fee for the introduction. This does not change what the firm charges you, and the firm must tell you about it in writing.

Nothing in these terms limits or excludes liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law, including liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or your statutory rights as a consumer under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
