Questions

Common questions

Will this affect my credit score?

No. Our form doesn't involve a credit check. If you decide to go ahead, a lender may run checks later on, and you should be told before they do.

Is Loanleaf a lender?

No. Loanleaf is a free introduction service, not a lender. We pass your enquiry to a finance specialist who can look at the options for your business.

How much can my business borrow?

From around £5,000 to over £2m. What's available depends on your business, its revenue and its credit history. Finance is subject to status.

Do I have to accept an offer?

No. You are under no obligation to go ahead with anything you are shown.

How is Loanleaf paid?

Our service is free for you. We receive a fee from the finance specialist we introduce you to. Any fees they or a lender charge should be explained before you agree to anything.

What happens to my information?

We only share your details with the finance specialist we match you with, and only if you agree. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details.
