Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how Leaflegal collects, uses and shares your personal information when you use leaflegal.co and our legal enquiry form. Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

1. Who we are

Leaflegal is a trading name of Vertical Brands Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 14254961. Registered office: 20–22 Wenlock Road, London, N1 7GU, United Kingdom. Email: hello@leaflegal.co.

We are the data controller for the personal information we collect through this website. This means we decide how and why it is used, and we are responsible for it under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Leaflegal is an introduction service. We are not a law firm, we do not give legal advice, and we are not regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA). With your permission, we pass your enquiry to one independent, regulated law firm that may be able to help you.

2. The information we collect

When you complete our enquiry form, we collect:

  • Contact details: your first and last name, email address and mobile number, and your business name if the enquiry is for a business.
  • Details about your matter: the area of law, the type of matter, how urgent it is, your postcode, whether you are able to pay privately for legal help, and a short summary if you choose to give one.
  • Records of your consent: the wording you agreed to (including the boxes you ticked) and when.

When you visit the website, we and our service providers also collect:

  • Technical and usage information: your IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, the website or advert that referred you, and campaign tags in the web address.
  • Cookie information: see our Cookie Policy.

We do not collect information from anyone under 18.

3. Sensitive information

Some legal matters can reveal sensitive information. A family law enquiry may reveal details about your relationships, health or children. An immigration enquiry may reveal your nationality or ethnic origin. A motoring offence enquiry relates to an alleged criminal offence. UK data protection law gives this information extra protection, as "special category" data (Article 9 UK GDPR) and "criminal offence" data (Article 10 UK GDPR).

  • We only use this information with your explicit consent, which you give by ticking the box on our form that lets us share your enquiry with a law firm. For criminal offence data we also rely on the condition in Schedule 1 of the Data Protection Act 2018 for processing with consent, and, where relevant, for the purpose of obtaining legal advice.
  • We ask only for what a firm needs to decide whether it can help. Please keep any summary short and do not include anything you would not want a law firm to read.
  • We never send details of your matter to advertising platforms such as Meta or LinkedIn.
  • We keep this information for a shorter time than other records (see section 9).

4. How we use your information

  • To review your enquiry and decide which law firm to introduce you to.
  • To pass your enquiry to that firm so it can contact you about your matter.
  • To contact you about your enquiry by email, text message or phone, for example to check your details or tell you which firm you have been matched with.
  • To check that enquiries are genuine and to prevent spam, fraud and misuse.
  • To understand how people use our website and adverts, and to improve them.
  • To keep records, deal with complaints and meet our legal obligations.

5. Our legal reasons for using your information

  • Consent: we share your enquiry with a law firm only if you tick the box agreeing to it. We use the Meta Pixel and the LinkedIn Insight Tag in your browser only if you accept marketing cookies. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 11, or use the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page).
  • Explicit consent: for special category and criminal offence data, as explained in section 3.
  • Legitimate interests: we use your contact details and technical information to run our service, check the quality of enquiries, measure how well our advertising works, prevent fraud, keep records and defend legal claims. We only do this where it does not override your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or share information.

6. Who we share your information with

The law firm we match you with. If you agree, we share your contact details and form answers with one law firm we select for your enquiry. The firm may contact you by phone, email or text message.

  • The firm is an independent business, not part of Leaflegal. It is regulated by the SRA, CILEx Regulation, the Council for Licensed Conveyancers or, for immigration only, the Immigration Advice Authority. You can check a solicitor on the SRA's register at sra.org.uk.
  • Once it receives your information, the firm is a separate data controller and is responsible for how it uses it, under its own privacy notice.
  • The firm decides whether it can take on your matter. If you instruct it, your relationship is with the firm, on its own terms of business.

Meta (advertising measurement). If you accept marketing cookies, we share limited information with Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd (which runs Facebook and Instagram) to measure our adverts. This is limited to the fact that a form was viewed or submitted, your IP address and browser details, any advert click ID and a hashed (scrambled) version of your email address and phone number. We never share the area of law, your matter or your answers.

LinkedIn (advertising measurement). If you accept marketing cookies, the LinkedIn Insight Tag tells LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company which pages you visit and when you submit an enquiry, along with your IP address and browser details. We don't send LinkedIn your name, contact details or anything about your matter.

Our service providers. These include website hosting, email delivery, phone and email checking services and advertising measurement providers. They act on our instructions and are not allowed to use your information for their own purposes.

Others, where necessary. These include our professional advisers, police, regulators, courts or other authorities where the law requires it, or a buyer of our business if Leaflegal or Vertical Brands Ltd is ever sold or restructured.

We do not sell your information to data brokers or list sellers, and we never share one enquiry with more than one firm at a time.

7. How we are paid

Our service is free to use. The law firm we introduce you to pays us a fee for the introduction. This does not change what the firm charges you. The firm must tell you about the fee in writing.

8. Transfers outside the UK

Some of our service providers store or process information outside the UK, for example in the European Economic Area or the United States. Where this happens, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK–US data bridge, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

9. How long we keep your information

We keep the details of your matter for up to 12 months after you last contacted us, and your contact details for up to 24 months. We keep records of your consent for up to 6 years, so that we can show what you agreed to if there is ever a dispute. We may keep information for longer where the law requires it. After that we securely delete it or make it anonymous.

10. Keeping your information secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limiting who can access it. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to deal promptly with any security incident.

11. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict or stop using your information;
  • object to us using your information for our legitimate interests, or for direct marketing;
  • ask us to send your information to you or another organisation in a reusable format; and
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

To use any of these rights, email hello@leaflegal.co. We will respond within one month. If you withdraw your consent after we have shared your enquiry, we will tell the firm you no longer want to be contacted. You should also contact the firm directly, because it controls its own copy of your information.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
