Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how Loanleaf collects, uses and shares your personal information when you use loanleaf.co and our business finance enquiry form. Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

1. Who we are

Loanleaf is a trading name of Vertical Brands Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 14254961. Registered office: 20–22 Wenlock Road, London, N1 7GU, United Kingdom. Email: hello@loanleaf.co.

We are the data controller for the personal information we collect through this website. This means we decide how and why it is used, and we are responsible for it under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Loanleaf is an introduction service. We are not a lender or a broker, we do not give financial advice, and we are not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). We pass your enquiry to a finance broker who may be able to help your business.

2. The information we collect

When you complete our enquiry form, we collect:

  • Contact details: your first and last name, business name, email address and mobile number.
  • Details about your funding needs: how much you want to borrow, what it is for and how soon you need it.
  • Details about your business: how it is set up, its annual revenue, how long it has been trading, whether there have been missed payments, defaults or County Court Judgments (CCJs) in the last 6 years, and whether it has existing loans or finance, including roughly how much is left to repay and the monthly repayments.
  • Records of your consent: the wording you agreed to (including any boxes you ticked) and when.

When you visit the website, we and our service providers also collect:

  • Technical and usage information: your IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, the website or advert that referred you, and campaign tags in the web address.
  • Cookie information: see our Cookie Policy.

Some of the answers you give, such as credit history and existing borrowing, may relate to you personally as a director or member of the business. Please only give information that is accurate and that you are entitled to share.

Our service is for businesses, not for children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

3. How we use your information

  • To review your enquiry and decide which broker to introduce you to.
  • To pass your enquiry to that broker so they can contact you about business finance.
  • To contact you about your enquiry by email, text message or WhatsApp, for example to check your details or tell you which broker you've been matched with.
  • To check that enquiries are genuine and to prevent spam, fraud and misuse.
  • To understand how people use our website and adverts, and to improve them.
  • To keep records, deal with complaints and meet our legal obligations.

4. Our legal reasons for using your information

  • Contract: when you send an enquiry you are asking us to introduce you to a broker, so we pass it to the broker we match you with so they can contact you about it.
  • Consent: we only use the Meta Pixel and the LinkedIn Insight Tag in your browser if you accept marketing cookies. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 10, or use the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page).
  • Legitimate interests: we use your information to run our service, check and improve the quality of enquiries, measure how well our advertising works (including through Meta’s Conversions API), prevent fraud, keep records and defend legal claims. We only do this where it doesn't override your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or share information.

5. Who we share your information with

The broker we match you with. If you agree, we share your contact details and form answers with a finance broker we select for your enquiry. The broker will usually contact you by phone, email or text message.

  • The broker is an independent business, not part of Loanleaf. Once it receives your information, the broker is responsible for how it uses it, under its own privacy notice.
  • The broker may or may not be authorised by the FCA. You can check a firm's status on the FCA Register at register.fca.org.uk.
  • The broker may share your information with lenders and may carry out credit and identity checks on your business and its directors. It should tell you about this before doing so.

Meta (advertising measurement). We share limited information with Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd (which runs Facebook and Instagram) to measure and improve our adverts. This includes events such as viewing or submitting a form, your IP address and browser details, any advert click ID and, when you submit an enquiry or application, a hashed (scrambled) version of your email address, phone number and name. We don’t share your answers about your business or its finances. Meta uses this information under its own terms and privacy policy.

LinkedIn (advertising measurement). If you accept marketing cookies, the LinkedIn Insight Tag tells LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company which pages you visit and when you submit an enquiry, along with your IP address and browser details, so we can measure and improve our LinkedIn adverts. We don’t send LinkedIn your name, contact details or your answers about your business. If you’re a broker who registers for or pays for a plan on Loanleaf Platform, we also tell LinkedIn about that from our server, with a hashed (scrambled) version of your email address, your name and your firm’s name, so we can measure our LinkedIn adverts. LinkedIn uses this information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Our service providers. These include website hosting, email delivery and analytics and advertising-tracking providers. They act on our instructions and are not allowed to use your information for their own purposes.

Others, where necessary. These include our professional advisers, police, regulators, courts or other authorities where the law requires it, or a buyer of our business if Loanleaf or Vertical Brands Ltd is ever sold or restructured.

We do not sell your information to data brokers or list sellers.

6. How we are paid

Our service is free to use. We may be paid by the broker we introduce you to. Any fees or charges a broker or lender asks you to pay are set by them and should be explained to you before you agree to anything.

7. Transfers outside the UK

Some of our service providers store or process information outside the UK, for example in the European Economic Area or the United States. Where this happens, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK–US data bridge, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

8. How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry information for up to 24 months after you last contacted us. We keep records of your consent for up to 6 years, so that we can show what you agreed to if there is ever a dispute. We may keep information for longer where the law requires it. After that we securely delete it or make it anonymous.

9. Keeping your information secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limiting who can access it. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to deal promptly with any security incident.

10. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict or stop using your information;
  • object to us using your information for our legitimate interests, or for direct marketing;
  • ask us to send your information to you or another organisation in a reusable format; and
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

To use any of these rights, email hello@loanleaf.co. We will respond within one month. If you withdraw your consent after we have shared your enquiry, we will tell the broker you no longer want to be contacted. You should also contact the broker directly, because it controls its own copy of your information.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
