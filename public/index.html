Questions

Is Leaflegal a law firm?
No. Leaflegal is a free introduction service. We pass your enquiry to one independent, regulated law firm, and their lawyers give you the advice. We don't give legal advice ourselves.
Does it cost anything?
Our service is free to use. We are paid by the law firm we introduce you to. The firm will explain its own fees before you agree to anything, and must tell you about the fee it pays us.
Who will contact me?
One law firm that handles your type of matter, usually by phone. We don't pass your details to a list of firms.
Which areas do you cover?
Conveyancing, family law, motoring offences, licensing, lease extensions and business debt recovery in England and Wales, and introductions to regulated immigration lawyers.
Can you help with legal aid?
We introduce people looking for paid legal help. If you think you may qualify for legal aid, GOV.UK has a checker and can point you to a legal aid adviser.
What happens to my information?
We only share your details with the firm we introduce you to, and only if you agree. We never share the details of your matter with advertisers. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details.
