Each step in detail

1Tell us what you need help with
Answer a few quick questions about the type of matter, how urgent it is and where you are. It takes about two minutes and costs nothing.
2We introduce you to one firm
We pass your enquiry to one regulated law firm that handles your type of matter and covers your area. Never to a list.
3The firm contacts you
The firm gets in touch, usually by phone, to understand your situation, explain how they can help and tell you their fees. There is no obligation to instruct them.

What happens after you submit

A clear timeline, so you always know what comes next.
Straight away

Your enquiry goes to the firm as soon as you submit it. You will see a confirmation on screen.

Usually the same working day

The firm calls or emails to introduce themselves and ask a few questions.

Before any work starts

The firm explains its fees in writing, including the fee it pays us, and what happens next.

Your choice

You decide whether to instruct the firm. The firm also decides whether it can take on your matter.

How we are paid

Leaflegal is free for you.

We are paid by the firm we introduce you to, so you never pay us. The firm must tell you about that fee in writing, and it does not change what the firm charges you.

Questions about how it works

How long does the form take?
About two minutes. It is a short set of multiple-choice questions, followed by your name and contact details.
How much does Leaflegal cost?
Nothing. Leaflegal is free to use. We are paid by the firm we introduce you to. The firm will explain its own fees before you agree to anything.
Who will contact me?
One law firm that handles your type of matter. We don’t pass your details to a list of firms.
Do I have to go ahead?
No. There is no obligation at any stage.
Will the firm definitely take my case?
Not always. The firm decides whether it can help once it understands your situation. If it can’t, it should tell you.
Is Leaflegal a law firm?
No. Leaflegal is an introduction service run by Vertical Brands Ltd. We don’t give legal advice and we are not regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority.
