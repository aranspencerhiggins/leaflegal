Business loan lead FAQs

Choose a topic to see the questions brokers ask us most. To see what a lead costs, try our cost per lead calculator.

What is Loanleaf?
Loanleaf is a business finance introduction service run by Vertical Brands Ltd. Business owners visit our website, answer a short set of questions about their funding needs and agree to be contacted by a finance broker. We then match each enquiry to one broker partner who arranges the type of finance the business is looking for.
Where can I buy business loan leads in the UK?
From Loanleaf. Sign up on Loanleaf Platform at platform.loanleaf.co, set the leads you want and choose a monthly plan, or apply to become a partner and our team will call you. Every lead is an exclusive, pre-screened enquiry from a UK limited company or LLP.
Who can become a partner?
We work with commercial finance brokers, asset finance brokers, commercial mortgage and bridging brokers, invoice and trade finance specialists, and lenders who fund UK businesses directly. If you arrange business finance for limited companies and LLPs, we would like to hear from you.
How do I become a partner?
Complete our partner application. It takes a couple of minutes and asks about the finance you arrange, the loan sizes you work with, your FCA status and how many leads you could handle each month. We will review it and get in touch to talk through your criteria, delivery and pricing.
How quickly can I start receiving leads?
Once we have agreed your criteria, pricing and delivery method and your partner agreement is in place, leads can start flowing as soon as matching enquiries come in. How many you receive depends on your criteria and the enquiries we are generating at the time.
Is there a long-term contract?
The terms of each partnership, including how long it runs and how it can be ended, are set out in your partner agreement before you receive your first lead. We would rather keep partners because the leads work for them than because they are tied in.
Can I see a sample lead?
Yes. When we talk through your application, we can show you exactly what a lead looks like, including the fields, the format and the consent record, before you commit to anything.
Are your commercial finance leads exclusive or shared?
Exclusive. Each enquiry is matched to one partner only. We don’t send the same lead to several brokers, and we don’t resell leads that have already been delivered.
What information is included in each lead?
Each lead includes the business owner’s name, the business name, a UK mobile number and an email address, plus their answers about how much they want to borrow, what it is for, how soon they need it, how the business is set up, its annual revenue, how long it has been trading, whether there have been missed payments, defaults or CCJs in the last 6 years, and any existing loans with the balance left to repay and monthly repayments. You also get the source, a timestamp and the consent record.
What criteria does every lead meet?
Every lead is a UK limited company or LLP that has been trading for at least 6 months, with annual revenue of £50,000 or more, looking for at least £5,000 in business finance. The business has given a valid UK mobile number and email address, and has agreed to be contacted by a broker.
Do you send leads from sole traders or start-ups?
No. Sole traders, ordinary partnerships and businesses that are not yet trading are told that we can’t help before their enquiry is submitted. This keeps leads focused on established companies that lenders are more likely to fund.
Can I choose which leads I receive?
Yes. You can choose the finance types, loan sizes and, where volumes allow, the sectors and regions you want. We only match enquiries to you that fit the criteria we have agreed together.
Can I filter leads by loan size?
Yes. Businesses choose an amount between £5,000 and £2m+ when they enquire. Set the range you work with in your auto-buy criteria, and filter the opportunities list in the same way.
Can I filter by credit history?
Every lead includes the business’s answer about missed payments, defaults or County Court Judgments in the last 6 years. If you only want clean-credit enquiries, you can switch that on in your auto-buy criteria.
How do you check contact details?
Our form only accepts a valid UK mobile number and checks that the email address is correctly formatted and on a domain that can receive email. It also suggests corrections for common email typos. These checks reduce wrong numbers and bounced emails, but they can’t guarantee that every business owner will answer.
Where do your leads come from?
All of our leads come from business owners completing the enquiry form on the Loanleaf website, usually after finding us through search or online advertising. We don’t buy in data, scrape directories or pass on cold contacts.
Are leads generated using paid advertising?
Some are. We use a mix of search, content and paid online advertising to bring business owners to our website. However they arrive, every lead completes the same form, meets the same criteria and gives the same consent.
What does a business see before it becomes a lead?
Business owners see a short, plain-English form that asks about their funding needs and their business. Before submitting, they agree to our terms and consent to being contacted by a broker we match them with. After submitting, they see a confirmation explaining that a finance specialist will review their details and call them.
Do you supply merchant cash advance (MCA) leads?
Yes. Businesses looking for cash flow or working capital are a natural fit for merchant cash advances. Choose the Cash flow purpose in your criteria to see those leads, or turn on auto-buy so you get them as soon as they arrive.
How is a lead different from a referral?
A referral usually comes from someone who knows the business and recommends you personally. A Loanleaf lead comes from a business owner who found us online, told us about their funding need and agreed to be contacted by a broker. There is no personal recommendation, but there is clear intent, full context and recorded consent.
How do you decide which partner gets a lead?
Each lead is matched to one partner whose agreed criteria it fits, taking into account finance type, loan size and any other filters, as well as each partner’s monthly cap. Where more than one partner could take a lead, we balance distribution fairly between them.
What do business owners expect when you call?
They expect a call from a finance specialist about the enquiry they have just made. They have been told a broker will review their details and get in touch to talk through their options, with no obligation to proceed.
How much do business loan leads cost?
Between £20 and £300. Each lead is priced by how fundable it is: how long the business has traded, its revenue, how much it wants and whether that is realistic for its size. You always see the price before you buy. Prices can change at any time without notice, so set a maximum price per lead if you use auto-buy. To see what a lead would cost, try our cost per lead calculator.
Do I pay a monthly fee?
Yes. Loanleaf works on monthly plans, starting at £2,500 a month + VAT, paid by card. Your plan adds credits every month, and you spend them on the leads you want. Unused credits roll over.
Is there a minimum number of leads I have to take?
No. The only minimum is the plan itself, £2,500 a month + VAT. You decide which leads to spend your credits on, whether that is a few large ones or many smaller ones.
Can I set a monthly cap?
Yes. On the platform you can set a monthly limit for auto-buy, in credits. Auto-buy never spends more than that limit or your balance.
Can I pause leads?
Yes. Switch auto-buy off whenever you like and buy leads manually, or cancel your plan from the billing page.
Do you guarantee a number of leads each month?
We can’t guarantee exact volumes, because lead flow depends on how many businesses enquire and how closely they match your criteria. We will give you an honest view of likely volumes when we agree your criteria, and you can set a monthly cap so you never receive more than you want.
How are leads delivered?
By email or straight into your CRM. Most CRMs can receive leads either through a dedicated lead email address or a webhook. We will work with you to set up whichever suits your team.
How quickly are leads delivered?
Leads are sent as soon as the business submits the enquiry form. Delivering in real time means you can call while the business owner is still thinking about their funding.
Which CRMs can you send leads to?
Any CRM that can receive leads by email or webhook. Let us know which system you use in your application and we will confirm how to connect it.
Can leads go to more than one person in my team?
Yes. We can send leads to a shared inbox or several email addresses, or into your CRM where you can assign them however you like.
What format do leads arrive in?
Email leads arrive as a clearly laid-out summary with every answer, the contact details, the source, the timestamp and the consent wording. CRM leads arrive as structured fields that map to your own lead records.
Do I need to be authorised by the FCA?
We work with brokers who may or may not be authorised by the FCA. You are responsible for meeting any regulatory requirements for the finance you arrange. Our leads are UK limited companies and LLPs looking for business finance.
Is Loanleaf authorised by the FCA?
No. Loanleaf is an introduction service. We don’t give advice, recommend products or arrange finance. We make this clear to business owners before they submit an enquiry.
Have the businesses agreed to be contacted?
Yes. Every business must tick a box agreeing to their details being shared with a broker we match them with, and to that broker contacting them by phone, email or text about business finance. We record the wording and the time, and include it with each lead.
Who is responsible for the data once I receive a lead?
Once a lead is delivered to you, you are the data controller for your copy and are responsible for handling it in line with UK data protection law and your own privacy notice. You should only use it to contact the business about their finance enquiry, and respect any request to stop contacting them.
Can I add leads to my marketing lists?
The business has agreed to be contacted about their business finance enquiry. Adding them to wider marketing needs its own lawful basis and, for electronic marketing, the right permissions. If in doubt, ask the business directly.
What happens if a business asks not to be contacted?
Stop contacting them and update your records. If they ask us, we will let you know so you can do the same.
What counts as a valid lead?
A valid lead has accurate contact details and matches the details shown before you bought it. A lead stays valid even if the business later decides not to proceed, chooses another provider or is declined by lenders.
What if a lead has wrong contact details?
Request a refund on the platform within 72 hours of buying the lead. If the phone number is not in service or the email address does not exist, the credits go back into your balance. We review requests within one working day. Refunds aren’t available for any other reason.
What if a lead doesn’t match my criteria?
You see every lead’s details before you buy, so manual purchases always match what you saw. If auto-buy picks something you didn’t expect, tighten your criteria on the Auto-buy page and the change applies to new leads straight away.
Will every lead turn into a deal?
No. As with any lead generation, not every enquiry will convert. Some businesses are comparing options, some won’t meet a lender’s criteria and some will decide not to borrow. We provide qualified enquiries, not guaranteed deals.
How can I improve my conversion rate?
Call quickly, use the information the business has already given you, explain how you were introduced, be clear about fees and follow up politely. Tracking each lead through your pipeline on the platform shows you which types of lead convert best.
What if a business has already found finance by the time I call?
It happens occasionally, especially if a lead is not called promptly. Calling within minutes of delivery is the best way to avoid it. A lead in this situation is still a valid lead, but tell us about it: patterns like this help us improve matching and timing.
Can I give feedback on individual leads?
Yes, and we encourage it. Telling us which leads converted, which didn’t and why is the quickest way to improve the quality and fit of the leads you receive.
Will I have a point of contact?
Yes. You will have a named contact at Loanleaf for questions about delivery, lead quality, your plan or your criteria.
Can I change my criteria later?
Yes. Update your loan sizes, revenue bands, purposes, trading history and monthly limit on the platform at any time. Changes apply to new leads straight away.
Do you work with lenders as well as brokers?
Yes. If you lend directly to UK businesses and want enquiries that fit your criteria, you are welcome to apply.
Do you offer leads outside the UK?
No. Our leads are UK limited companies and LLPs only.
Can you help with our own marketing?
Loanleaf is run by Vertical Brands Ltd, a marketing agency. If you want help with your own website, paid media or lead generation, mention it in your application and we can talk about how we could help.
Do you work with brokers across the whole of the UK?
Yes. Partners can be based anywhere in the UK. If you only work with businesses in certain regions, tell us and we will take that into account where our volumes allow.
How do I get in touch?
The quickest way is to complete the partner application. You can also email us at hello@loanleaf.co.
