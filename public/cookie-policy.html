Cookie Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

This policy explains how Leaflegal uses cookies and similar technologies on leaflegal.co. Leaflegal is a trading name of Vertical Brands Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 14254961. Registered office at 20–22 Wenlock Road, London, N1 7GU, United Kingdom. Email hello@leaflegal.co.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work in the same way. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

2. Your choices

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept analytics and marketing cookies. You can accept all, reject all, or choose each type. Only essential cookies are used unless you say yes.

You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

3. The cookies we use

Essential (always on). These are needed for the site and enquiry form to work, so they don't need your consent.

  • ll-cookies remembers your cookie choices. Kept until you change them or clear your browser.
  • ll-apply-v2 and lg-partner-enquiry-v1 save your answers while you fill in a form, so you don't lose them if the page reloads. Cleared when you submit.

Advertising measurement. Used to link your enquiry to the advert you clicked.

  • ll-fbc if you arrive from one of our Facebook or Instagram adverts, remembers the advert click ID for your visit so your enquiry can be linked to that advert. Cleared when you close your browser.

Analytics (only with your consent). These help us understand how people use the site, such as which pages they visit, so we can improve it.

Marketing (only with your consent). These help us measure how well our adverts work and show you relevant adverts on other websites.

  • Meta Pixel (_fbp, _fbc) set by Meta when you accept marketing cookies. Lets Meta measure visits and enquiries from our Facebook and Instagram adverts and show you relevant adverts. Kept for up to 90 days.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag (li_fat_id, li_sugr, bcookie, lidc, UserMatchHistory, AnalyticsSyncHistory) set by LinkedIn when you accept marketing cookies. Lets LinkedIn measure visits and enquiries from our LinkedIn adverts and show you relevant adverts. Kept for up to 2 years.

We don't currently use any analytics cookies. See our Privacy Policy for how we use your information.

4. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

5. Contact us

If you have any questions, email hello@leaflegal.co. For more about how we use personal information, see our Privacy Policy.
