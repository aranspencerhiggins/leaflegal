How it works

Leaflegal generates exclusive family law enquiries from people and businesses who have asked to speak to a solicitor. Divorce, finances on separation and child arrangements.

What family law enquiries look like

People going through a separation want to understand their options quickly and usually want to speak to someone the same day. Since legal aid for most private family matters was withdrawn, most clients pay privately, which makes family enquiries some of the most valuable we generate.

What’s in every lead

  • Divorce, finances, children or other
  • Whether proceedings have started
  • Urgency and any hearing date
  • Paid help confirmed
  • Location
  • Verified UK mobile and email
  • Recorded consent to be contacted

Matter types you can choose

Choose the matters you want and leave out the ones you don’t.

  • Divorce leads
  • Financial settlements leads
  • Consent orders leads
  • Child arrangements leads
  • Separation agreements leads
  • Cohabitation disputes leads
  • Prenuptial agreements leads
  • Ending a civil partnership leads

Who these leads suit

Family practices from fixed fee divorce through to complex financial remedy and children work.

Every client has confirmed they want paid help, not legal aid.

Pricing and volume

You pay for each lead we deliver to you, at a price agreed with you before you start. There are no shared or recycled enquiries. You know exactly what each lead costs, so you can measure your return against the instructions you win.

You stay in control of volume. The minimum is 10 leads per month; above that, tell us how many leads your team can handle each month and we will match to that number, so you are never flooded with enquiries you can’t call back quickly. If you want more, or need to pause, just let us know.

Questions

Are family law leads exclusive?
Yes. Each enquiry goes to one firm only and is never resold.
Can I choose locations for family law leads?
Yes. Tell us the towns, counties or regions you cover, or national.
Are the leads exclusive?
Yes. Each enquiry is matched to one firm only. We don’t send the same lead to several firms and we don’t resell leads that have already been delivered.
What is included in each lead?
The client’s name, a UK mobile number and an email address, the practice area and type of matter, a short description in their own words, how urgent it is, where they are and the best time to call. You also get the source, a timestamp and the consent record.
Where do your leads come from?
From people completing our own enquiry forms, usually after seeing one of our campaigns on social media or finding us through search. We don’t buy in data, scrape directories or pass on cold contacts.
