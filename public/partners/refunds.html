You can request a refund within 72 hours of buying a lead, but only if the phone number is not in service or belongs to someone else, or the email address does not exist. There are no refunds for any other reason.

The policy in one place

Loanleaf checks leads before sale, but a number can still turn out to be unreachable. The refund policy covers that one problem: invalid contact details. Everything else about the lead, including whether the owner answers or goes ahead, is your commercial risk as the buyer.

Refunded leads return credits to your balance, so you can spend them on another lead.

The rule is deliberately narrow. Loanleaf can check that a number is a valid UK mobile and that an email domain can receive mail, but it cannot promise that every owner will pick up. The refund covers the part Loanleaf can stand behind.

What qualifies

A request qualifies if, within 72 hours of purchase, one of these is true.

  • The phone number is not in service.
  • The phone number belongs to someone else.
  • The email address does not exist.

These are about wrong contact details, not about the lead’s quality or what the owner said.

What does not qualify

No refunds are given for any other reason. Examples that do not qualify:

  • The owner does not answer, or you cannot reach them after a few calls.
  • The owner has changed their mind or is just exploring options.
  • The business does not suit your lenders, or the credit history is poor.
  • The details on the form are different from what you hear on the call.
  • The owner is also talking to another firm.
  • You bought the lead by mistake or the price was higher than you expected.
  • The request is made after 72 hours.

Read the lead properly before you buy. The summary shows amount, purpose, timeline, revenue, time trading, credit history, existing finance, the quality score and the price.

How to request a refund

Request it from the platform, from the lead in My leads. Say which of the three reasons applies, for example “phone not in service”, and add a short note about what you tried. The team reviews requests and, if the details are invalid, returns the credits.

Make the request promptly. Do not wait until day three to find out. Call the number and send a test email as soon as the lead arrives.

Timing

The 72 hours start when you buy the lead, and the request has to be made within that time. Requests are reviewed by the team, usually within one working day. Once approved, the credits go back to your balance.

Getting good evidence

You will make a stronger request if you can show what happened. For a phone, note the time and number of attempts, and what you heard, such as a recorded message saying the number is not in service. If you reached someone who says they are not the owner, note that. For an email, keep the bounce message.

Also check that the number or address in your CRM matches the one on the platform, since a mismatch caused by your own webhook mapping is not an invalid lead.

Keep your notes short and factual. The aim is to let the team see quickly that the contact details were invalid, not to argue about the lead.

A worked example

You buy a lead at 10am on Monday and ring the number straight away. The line says the number is not in service. You try again that afternoon and the next morning, with the same result. You open the lead in My leads, request a refund with the reason “phone not in service”, and note your attempts. Provided the request is in before 10am on Thursday, it qualifies.

Compare that with a lead where the phone rings out. The number is valid, so there is nothing to refund. Keep trying at different times of day, and send an email as well.

Reducing the chance of a refund

Many problems can be spotted before you buy. Look for the “valid UK mobile” and “domain verified” ticks, read the quality score and review the Companies House summary. Auto-buy never takes a lead whose phone or email check failed. More on this in lead verification.

For how credits work, read the pricing page.

Finally, call promptly. A number that fails in the first hour is much easier to evidence than one that you only try on day three, and a quick call also tells you whether you have a lead worth chasing.

Questions

How long do I have to ask for a refund?
72 hours from the time you bought the lead.
Can I get a refund if the owner will not answer?
No. The number has to be not in service or belong to someone else, or the email must not exist. A valid number that is not picked up does not qualify.
What happens to refunded credits?
They are returned to your credit balance, and you can use them on another lead.
Can I get a refund for a lead I do not like the look of?
No. You see the lead summary and price before buying. Refunds are only for invalid contact details.
Does the refund apply to auto-buy leads?
Yes. The rule is the same however the lead was bought, and the 72 hours run from the purchase.
Is the refund policy part of the partner agreement?
The refund rules apply as set out when you sign up and in your partner agreement. Read the agreement during registration, and ask the team through the partner application if you want it explained first.
