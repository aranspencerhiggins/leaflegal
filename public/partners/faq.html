Questions and answers

Everything firms ask about Leaflegal leads.

What is Leaflegal?
Leaflegal is an introduction service run by Vertical Brands Ltd. We run marketing campaigns for people and businesses who need legal help, ask them a short set of questions about their matter and, with their consent, introduce each one to a single partner firm that handles that type of work.
Which practice areas do you cover?
Conveyancing, family law, debt recovery for businesses, motoring offences, licensing, lease extensions and immigration. You choose which of these you want to receive.
Who can become a partner firm?
SRA-regulated solicitors’ firms, CILEx-regulated practices, CLC-regulated conveyancers and, for immigration, advisers registered with the Immigration Advice Authority (formerly the OISC). You must be authorised for the work you take on.
How do I apply?
Complete the partner application. It takes a couple of minutes and asks about your practice areas, how you are regulated, the areas you cover and how many leads you want each month. We will then call you to agree your criteria, delivery and pricing.
How quickly can I start?
Once your criteria, pricing and delivery are agreed and your partner agreement is in place, leads start as soon as matching enquiries come in. Volumes depend on your practice areas and locations.
Can I see a sample lead?
Yes. When we talk through your application, we will show you exactly what a lead looks like, including the fields, the format and the consent record, before you commit to anything.
Are the leads exclusive?
Yes. Each enquiry is matched to one firm only. We don’t send the same lead to several firms and we don’t resell leads that have already been delivered.
What is included in each lead?
The client’s name, a UK mobile number and an email address, the practice area and type of matter, a short description in their own words, how urgent it is, where they are and the best time to call. You also get the source, a timestamp and the consent record.
Where do your leads come from?
From people completing our own enquiry forms, usually after seeing one of our campaigns on social media or finding us through search. We don’t buy in data, scrape directories or pass on cold contacts.
Can I choose locations?
Yes. Tell us the towns, counties or regions you cover, or that you work nationally and remotely, and we only send you enquiries from those areas.
Can I choose specific matter types?
Yes. Within each practice area you can tell us the matters you want and the ones you don’t. For example, you might want contested divorce finances but not uncontested divorce, or purchases but not remortgages.
Do you send legal aid enquiries?
No. Every lead has confirmed they are looking for paid legal help. People who only want legal aid or free advice are pointed to the right place before their enquiry is submitted.
Do you cover Scotland and Northern Ireland?
Not at the moment. Our legal leads are for matters in England and Wales, apart from immigration introductions, which can come from anywhere in the UK.
How do you decide which firm gets a lead?
Each lead goes to one firm whose practice areas, matter types and locations it fits, taking into account each firm’s monthly cap. Where more than one firm could take a lead, we balance distribution fairly between them.
How much does each lead cost?
Pricing is agreed with each firm before it starts and depends on the practice areas, matter types and locations you choose. Apply and we will put a proposal together for you.
Is there a minimum number of leads?
Yes. The minimum is 10 leads per month. Above that, tell us how many leads you want and we will match to that number, based on your criteria and the enquiries we are generating.
Can I set a monthly cap?
Yes. You tell us how many leads you want each month and we stop matching to you once you reach that number. You can change it at any time.
Can I pause leads?
Yes. If your team is at capacity or a fee earner is away, tell us and we will pause matching until you are ready again.
Do you guarantee a number of leads?
We can’t guarantee exact volumes, because they depend on how many people enquire and how closely they match your criteria. We will give you an honest view of likely volumes when we agree your criteria.
How are leads delivered?
By email or straight into your case management system or CRM, using a lead email address or a webhook. We will set up whichever suits your intake team.
How quickly are leads delivered?
As soon as the enquiry is submitted, so you can call while the client is still thinking about their matter.
Can leads go to more than one person?
Yes. We can send leads to a shared intake inbox, several email addresses or your CRM, where you can assign them to the right fee earner.
Is Leaflegal a law firm?
No. Leaflegal is an introduction service. We don’t give legal advice and we are not regulated by the SRA. We make this clear to every client before they submit an enquiry.
Do I need to disclose the referral to the client?
Yes. The SRA Code of Conduct requires you to make sure clients are told about any financial arrangement you have with an introducer. Our consent wording tells clients we are paid by the firms we introduce them to, and we send you that wording with each lead.
Why don’t you offer personal injury leads?
Paying referral fees for personal injury and clinical negligence work is prohibited under section 56 of LASPO, so we don’t generate them.
Can you send immigration leads to non-solicitor advisers?
Only to advisers registered with the Immigration Advice Authority (formerly the OISC) at the right level for the work, or to firms regulated by the SRA or CILEx Regulation. Leaflegal never gives immigration advice itself.
Have the clients agreed to be contacted?
Yes. Every client ticks a box agreeing to their details being shared with a firm we match them with, and to that firm contacting them by phone, email or text about their matter. We record the wording and the time.
Who is responsible for the data once I receive a lead?
You become the data controller for your copy and are responsible for handling it in line with UK data protection law and your own privacy notice. You should only use it to contact the client about their enquiry.
What about conflicts of interest?
Run your normal conflict checks before taking on a matter. If you find a conflict, don’t act and let us know, and we will treat the lead as your partner agreement sets out.
What counts as a valid lead?
A valid lead has accurate contact details and matches the practice areas, matter types and locations agreed with you. It stays valid if the client later decides not to instruct you or chooses another firm.
What if a lead has wrong contact details?
Let us know within the review period in your partner agreement. Where a lead is genuinely invalid, we will deal with it as your agreement sets out.
Will every lead become an instruction?
No. Some people are comparing firms, some decide not to proceed and some can’t afford the fees. We provide qualified enquiries, not guaranteed instructions.
How can I convert more leads?
Call quickly, use what the client has already told you, explain how you were introduced, be clear about fees and follow up politely. Telling us which leads convert also helps us refine your criteria.
