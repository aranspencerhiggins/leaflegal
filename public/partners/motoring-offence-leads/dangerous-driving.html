How it works

Exclusive dangerous driving enquiries from people who have asked to speak to a solicitor. Dangerous driving cases are serious, privately funded matters that justify substantial fees.

What the client is looking for

Representation for serious driving charges.

Dangerous driving is a serious offence, with an obligatory ban and an extended retest, and it can carry a prison sentence. Charges are often heard in the Crown Court.

What the client expects from you

  • Advice at the police station and after charge
  • Reviewing evidence and expert reports
  • Representation in the magistrates’ and Crown Court
  • Mitigation
  • Related offences

What’s in every lead

  • Type of offence
  • Court date if known
  • Current points
  • Whether a ban is likely
  • Location
  • Verified UK mobile and email
  • Recorded consent to be contacted

Compliance

Clients have confirmed they want paid representation.

Every client is told Leaflegal is an introducer paid by the firms it introduces, and we send you the consent wording with each lead.

Pricing

Pay per lead at a price agreed with you. The minimum is 10 leads a month across your chosen matters, and you can pause at any time.

Leaflegal is a trading name of Vertical Brands Ltd and an introducer, not a law firm. It is not regulated by the SRA and does not give legal or immigration advice. Partner firms are responsible for their own regulatory position.
