Exclusive leads reward the broker who calls first, because the business owner has just asked for help and is expecting a call. This guide covers the practical setup on Loanleaf Platform: email alerts, auto-buy, CRM webhooks and call windows.

Why speed matters with exclusive leads

With an exclusive lead, you are not racing other brokers on the same enquiry. Each lead is sold to one broker only, and once bought it disappears for everyone else. So why does speed still matter?

Because the business owner is. They have just completed a form about their funding need and ticked a box agreeing to be contacted. They are at their most engaged in the minutes afterwards. Every hour that passes, the call becomes less expected, and the chance of them being busy, distracted or already talking to someone else goes up.

Speed is also about the stage of the lead's life. A lead that arrives, is bought and is called within minutes gets a warm conversation. A lead called the next morning is a cold call to someone who barely remembers asking.

Two delays to remove

There are two gaps between a business owner submitting the form and you speaking to them. The first is the time until you own the lead. The second is the time between owning it and dialling.

Auto-buy removes most of the first gap, because it buys matching leads the moment they arrive. Alerts, webhooks and a clear process remove most of the second. Most brokers lose time in the second gap, not the first, because a lead sits in an inbox waiting for someone to notice it.

Fix the gap you actually have. If you buy manually and check the platform twice a day, the first gap is your problem. If you auto-buy but leads wait for a morning review, the second is.

Alerts for manual buyers

If you buy manually, switch on the email for matching leads. You get an email when a lead arrives that fits your criteria, so you do not need to keep the platform open.

Make that email reach the right person. Send it to a shared inbox or to the phone of whoever is on call, not to a general address that is read at the end of the day.

When the alert arrives, open the lead on the Opportunities page, check the amount, purpose, timeline, revenue, trading time, credit history, existing finance and score, and decide quickly. The masked contact details show whether the phone is a valid UK mobile and the email domain is verified, so you know the lead is reachable before you spend credits.

Auto-buy for the fastest first contact

Auto-buy buys leads that match your criteria the moment they arrive. You set the loan size range, revenue bands, purposes, time trading and whether to accept clean credit only. You also set limits: leads per day, credits per day, credits per month and the maximum credits per lead.

It will never buy a lead whose phone or email check failed. If several brokers match the same lead, a fair rotation decides who gets it, so being fast is not about out-clicking anyone. It is about being ready when the lead lands in your account.

Leads above your per-lead maximum are left for manual buying, and you get an email alert. That keeps a cap on spend without losing sight of larger leads. Our page on auto-buy covers each setting.

Webhook to your CRM

Bought leads are delivered instantly on the platform, by email and by webhook. The webhook sends the lead as JSON to your CRM, and works with Zapier, Make, HubSpot, Pipedrive and any CRM that accepts webhooks. You can add up to 5 webhooks.

The aim is that the lead lands where your team already works, with a task or a notification attached. A lead that has to be copied from an email into a system by hand will be called later than one that appears with the owner's name and number ready to dial.

Test the webhook before you rely on it. Buy or receive a lead, check every field mapped correctly, and confirm the right person is assigned. It is a few minutes of setup that saves lost leads later.

Call windows and who picks up

Decide in advance who calls and when. A named person with a clear window is better than a shared inbox everyone assumes someone else is watching.

Think about when business owners are reachable. Many are easier to reach early in the morning, at lunchtime or early evening, outside their busiest working hours. You know your own market, so test your own pattern rather than relying on a rule of thumb.

For leads that arrive outside working hours, set a rule: the first call is made at the start of the next window, and the lead is flagged so it is first in the queue. If a lead is marked as needed as soon as possible, consider whether someone can take it sooner.

What to do in the first ten minutes

Have a short routine so nothing slows you down once the lead lands.

  • Read the summary: amount, purpose, timeline, credit answer and existing finance.
  • Note what the form did not ask, such as security, card takings or sector, so you can cover it on the call.
  • Call the mobile. If there is no answer, leave a short voicemail that says who you are and that they asked about finance.
  • Send a brief email or text straight afterwards, so they have your name and number.

The routine for the call itself is in our first call script, and what to do when nobody picks up is in the guide to following up quiet leads.

Questions

How fast should I call a new lead?
As soon as you can. There is no magic number, but the owner has just submitted the form and is expecting to be contacted. Aim to remove every delay you control, from buying to dialling.
Does auto-buy make me faster than other brokers?
Leads are exclusive, so you are not competing with other brokers once you own one. Auto-buy removes the delay before you own the lead. If several brokers match, a fair rotation decides who gets it.
What if a lead arrives out of hours?
Set a rule so it is called at the start of the next call window and sits at the top of the queue. You can also send a short email or text so the owner knows who will be calling.
Can I send leads to more than one CRM?
You can add up to 5 webhooks, so leads can reach more than one system. Each bought lead is also sent by email and shown on the platform.
Do I have to call, or can I email first?
The owner consented to contact by phone, email or text. A call is usually the quickest way to qualify, with a follow-up email or text straight after. You remain responsible for your own regulatory position.
