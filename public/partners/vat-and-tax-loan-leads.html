What Loanleaf offers brokers who place VAT and tax loans

A tax bill is a deadline with a clear amount attached. The owner knows roughly what is due and roughly when, which makes it one of the more concrete reasons to borrow. That is also why VAT loan leads are time-sensitive: the owner who asks today may have sorted it by next week.

Loanleaf is an introducer. Business owners find us through search, our own content and paid online advertising, then complete an enquiry form on our website. Those who pass the form are offered for sale to one broker. We do not buy in data, scrape lists or pass on cold contacts.

This page explains how to find tax-related enquiries on Loanleaf Platform, what each lead tells you, what it does not, and how leads are priced. It is written for brokers and lenders who already arrange tax funding and want a reliable source of owners who have asked for it.

How tax enquiries appear on Loanleaf

When a business owner completes the enquiry form, they choose the purpose of the borrowing from a fixed list. One of those options is Tax or VAT bill. Every lead with that purpose is a business owner who told us, in their own words and unprompted by any product, that a tax bill is why they want finance.

The same form also asks how much they want to borrow, how soon they need it, how the company is structured, annual revenue, how long it has been trading, whether there have been missed payments, defaults or CCJs in the last six years, and whether any finance is already in place. All of that is visible to you on the lead summary before you buy.

That is usually enough to decide quickly whether a tax enquiry is worth a call. A £40,000 request from a company with £1m to £5m revenue and five years of trading looks different from the same request on £100k revenue.

Where the leads come from and who qualifies

Every enquiry is filtered on the form before it is submitted. Businesses that do not meet the basics are turned away at that point, so they never reach the platform and you never pay for them.

  • UK limited companies and LLPs only. Sole traders, ordinary partnerships and businesses not yet trading are declined.
  • At least 6 months trading and annual revenue of £50,000 or more.
  • A loan amount chosen from £5,000 to £2m+.
  • A valid UK mobile number and an email domain that can receive email, both checked on the form.
  • No paying twice: a repeat enquiry within 7 days goes back to the broker who bought it at no extra cost, and after that you never receive the same lead again.

Before submitting, the owner confirms that they accept our terms and privacy policy, understand that Loanleaf is an introducer and not a lender or broker, and consent to their details being shared with a matched broker who may contact them by phone, email or text about business finance. The exact wording and a timestamp are recorded and supplied with the lead.

What you can and cannot see in VAT loan leads

The purpose field tells you that a tax bill is involved. It does not tell you which one. The options are grouped as a single choice, Tax or VAT bill, so the same label covers a quarterly VAT payment, a corporation tax liability, a PAYE bill or anything else the owner thinks of as tax.

The form also does not ask for the amount owed separately from the amount borrowed, the date the bill falls due, whether the owner has already agreed a payment arrangement, or whether the company has filed on time. It does not ask for sector, director names or bank statements either.

None of that is hidden. It is simply what a short form leaves for the first call. A good opening question is which tax the bill relates to and when it is due. The answer shapes everything that follows, including which lender or product suits and how quickly you will need to move.

Corporation tax loan leads and other tax bills

Corporation tax loan leads arrive through exactly the same route as VAT enquiries. The owner picks Tax or VAT bill, and the lead then needs a conversation to find out which liability is behind it. Some brokers keep a short script for this so that every tax lead is sorted in the first minute of the call.

A corporation tax bill tends to be one larger payment, and companies with a bill like that are often profitable and established, which can make them more straightforward to place. A VAT bill can recur, which may make a short-term facility or a repeatable arrangement more relevant. Treat that as a starting hypothesis, not a rule: the owner's circumstances decide.

If you only handle one kind of tax funding, you will need to accept that some Tax or VAT bill leads will not fit. Price that into how you buy, and consider whether a broader approach, taking all tax leads and passing those you cannot place to another firm, makes sense for your business.

How tax loan leads are priced

Loanleaf prices every lead in credits, where 1 credit is £1 of lead value. Each lead costs between £20 and £300, set by a scoring model that looks at loan size, revenue, time trading, credit history and affordability, meaning whether the amount asked for is realistic for the business's revenue.

That model matters for tax enquiries, because the amount borrowed is tied to a bill rather than to ambition. A sensible ask from an established business costs more, and an ask far out of proportion to revenue costs the minimum. Examples from the live model:

  • A £25k loan, £100k to £250k revenue, 1 to 2 years trading: 40 credits.
  • A £100k loan, £250k to £500k revenue, 2 to 5 years trading: 95 credits.
  • A £250k loan, £1m to £5m revenue, 5+ years trading: 165 credits.
  • A £5m ask from a business with £100k to £250k revenue and 6 to 12 months trading: 15 credits.

Loanleaf is a marketplace, so prices move with demand and lead quality and can change at any time without notice. You always see the price before you buy, and in auto-buy you can set a maximum price per lead so it never spends more than you intend.

Setting up Loanleaf Platform for tax enquiries

Leads are sold on Loanleaf Platform, a self-serve portal. Before buying, you see a summary of every live lead: amount, purpose, timeline, revenue, trading time, credit history, existing finance and a quality score from 0 to 100. Contact details are masked, with checks for a valid UK mobile and a verified email domain. The business name and full contact details unlock when you buy.

Tax enquiries are time-sensitive, so many brokers switch on auto-buy. It buys matching leads the moment they arrive, and if several brokers match, a fair rotation decides who gets it. A simple tax-focused setup might be:

  • Purposes: Tax or VAT bill only, or with Cash flow if you fund both.
  • Loan size range: the amounts you can place, for example the lower and middle part of the range.
  • Time trading and revenue: bands that match what your lenders accept.
  • Credit: clean credit only, if your lenders are cautious about adverse history.
  • Limits: leads per day, credits per day, credits per month and a maximum credits per lead.

Leads above your per-lead maximum are left for manual buying and you get an email alert. If you prefer to choose for yourself, manual-mode brokers can ask for an email when a matching lead arrives. The auto-buy page explains each setting.

Working a tax lead on the first call

Bought leads are delivered instantly, on the platform, by email and to your CRM by webhook as JSON. That works with Zapier, Make, HubSpot, Pipedrive and any CRM that accepts webhooks. For an owner with a deadline, the first hour matters.

The call should confirm what the form leaves out, then move quickly to whether you can help. A short list covers most of it.

  • Which tax is owed, how much and by what date.
  • Whether the owner has already spoken to HMRC about the bill.
  • How the business plans to repay: from trading cash flow, over a set period or from a known receipt.
  • What existing finance is in place and whether it limits further borrowing.

The pipeline on Loanleaf Platform has the stages New, Contacted, Application, Funded and Lost, with notes and CSV export. Recording the type of tax against each lead is a simple way to learn which kinds you convert.

Plans, refunds and getting started

Loanleaf works on monthly subscription plans, and prices exclude VAT. Starter is £2,500 a month for 2,500 credits, Growth is £5,000 a month for 5,000 credits, and Custom plans start from £6,000 in £1,000 steps. One credit is always £1, with no bonus credits, and plans over 20,000 credits a month are arranged with the team. Unused credits roll over while you are subscribed and expire when the subscription ends. You can cancel any time, upgrades take effect immediately and downgrades apply at renewal.

Refunds are available within 72 hours of purchase, but only if the phone number is not in service or belongs to someone else, or the email address does not exist. There are no refunds for any other reason. See the pricing page for the full picture.

To start, sign up at platform.loanleaf.co/register. You add your company details and lead criteria, choose a plan, accept the partner agreement online and pay by card, which takes about five minutes. Loanleaf checks each firm before it can buy leads, usually within one working day. If you would rather talk first, you can apply to become a partner. Questions

What are VAT loan leads?
They are enquiries from UK limited companies and LLPs whose owners chose Tax or VAT bill as the purpose of borrowing on the Loanleaf website. Each one includes the amount, timeline, revenue, trading history, credit history and existing finance, plus contact details that unlock when you buy.
Can I tell whether a lead is VAT, corporation tax or PAYE?
No. The form has a single option, Tax or VAT bill, and does not ask which tax is owed or when it is due. You confirm that on the first call, so VAT loan leads and corporation tax loan leads arrive under the same purpose.
Are tax loan leads exclusive?
Yes. Each lead is sold to one broker only. Once you buy it, it disappears for everyone else and is never resold. If the same business enquires again within 7 days, the repeat goes back to the broker who bought it at no extra cost. After that, you never receive the same lead again and never pay for the same lead twice.
How much does a tax loan lead cost?
Between £20 and £300 in credits, where 1 credit is £1. The price is set by loan size, revenue, time trading, credit history and affordability, and you see it before you buy. Prices can change without notice, so set a maximum price per lead if you use auto-buy.
Can I filter for tax leads only?
Yes. In auto-buy you can choose the purposes you want, alongside loan size range, revenue bands, time trading and clean credit only. Add limits for leads per day, credits per day, credits per month and a maximum credits per lead.
Does the form ask whether the owner has an HMRC payment arrangement?
No. It does not ask about payment arrangements, filing history or due dates. It also does not ask about sector, director names or bank statements. Brokers qualify those points on the first call.
How fast are leads delivered?
Instantly on purchase. A bought lead appears on the platform, arrives by email and is sent to your CRM by webhook as JSON, which works with Zapier, Make, HubSpot, Pipedrive and any CRM that accepts webhooks.
What if the contact details are wrong?
You can request a refund within 72 hours of purchase if the phone number is not in service or belongs to someone else, or the email address does not exist. No refunds are given for any other reason, including a business deciding not to borrow.
