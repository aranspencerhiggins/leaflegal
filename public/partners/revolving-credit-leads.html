What Loanleaf offers revolving credit brokers and lenders

A revolving facility suits a business that borrows and repays in cycles: paying suppliers before customers pay, covering a seasonal dip, or keeping a buffer for the weeks when cash is tight. The brokers who place these facilities need a steady supply of owners with that pattern, and they need to reach them while the need is live.

Loanleaf is an introducer. Business owners find us through search, our own content and paid online advertising, then complete an enquiry form on our website. Each enquiry that passes the form is offered for sale to one broker. We do not buy in data, scrape lists or pass on cold contacts.

This page explains which Loanleaf enquiries tend to suit a revolving credit facility, how the leads are qualified and priced, and how to set up Loanleaf Platform so that you see mostly the ones you can place. It also says plainly what the form does not ask, so you can decide whether it fits the way you work.

What a revolving credit facility lead looks like

Loanleaf does not run a separate form for revolving credit. Instead, every lead carries a set of answers that let you pick out the enquiries that look like a facility fit. Business owners choose what the money is for from a fixed list, and the purposes closest to a revolving need are Cash flow, Stock and inventory, and Growth and expansion.

Alongside the purpose, each lead shows the amount asked for, how soon the owner needs it, the company structure, the revenue band, the time trading band, credit history and any existing finance. For a lender deciding on a credit line, those fields are the first things you would ask about anyway.

Where the leads come from and who qualifies

Every enquiry is filtered on the form before it is submitted. Businesses that do not meet the basics are turned away at that point, so they never reach the platform and you never pay for them.

  • UK limited companies and LLPs only. Sole traders, ordinary partnerships and businesses not yet trading are declined.
  • At least 6 months trading and annual revenue of £50,000 or more.
  • A loan amount chosen from £5,000 to £2m+.
  • A valid UK mobile number and an email domain that can receive email, both checked on the form.
  • No paying twice: a repeat enquiry within 7 days goes back to the broker who bought it at no extra cost, and after that you never receive the same lead again.

Before submitting, the owner confirms that they accept our terms and privacy policy, understand that Loanleaf is an introducer and not a lender or broker, and consent to their details being shared with a matched broker who may contact them by phone, email or text about business finance. The exact wording and a timestamp are recorded and supplied with the lead.

Which leads tend to suit a business credit line

Brokers who place business credit line leads usually look for a handful of signals on the summary. None is a guarantee, but together they narrow a long list quickly.

  • Purpose: Cash flow. The closest match to how most revolving facilities are used. Stock and inventory is also worth a look, particularly for businesses that buy ahead of a busy season.
  • Time trading: 2 to 5 years or 5+ years. Established businesses with a longer record are generally easier to place on a facility than very young ones.
  • Revenue band: £250k and above. A facility has to be sized sensibly against trading, and higher bands give you more room to work with.
  • Existing finance. Each lead says whether the business already has finance, how much is left to repay and the monthly repayment band. An owner with a term loan and a cash flow need may be looking to add a facility, or to replace something more expensive.
  • Credit history. Each lead shows whether there have been missed payments, defaults or CCJs in the last 6 years (No, Yes or Not sure). Your lenders' appetite will decide how much weight to give it.

The urgency field helps too. An owner who needs money as soon as possible and one who is just exploring options are very different conversations, and you can see which is which before you spend a credit.

The honest limits of these leads

The Loanleaf form is short by design, and there are things it does not ask. It does not ask for a property value or loan to value, the address or type of any property, planning status, sector, director names or bank statements. It also does not ask about card takings or card machine turnover.

For a revolving credit facility, that has practical consequences. The form cannot tell you what security a business could offer, how lumpy its receivables are or how much of its revenue is seasonal. It also cannot say whether the owner understands the difference between a facility they draw on and repay, and a one-off loan.

You establish all of that on the first call. Some cash flow enquiries will turn out to suit a term loan, invoice finance or a merchant cash advance better than a facility. If your panel can place those products, the lead still has value. If you only write revolving facilities, expect a share of enquiries not to fit and allow for it in how you buy.

How RCF leads are priced

Loanleaf prices every lead in credits, where 1 credit is £1 of lead value. Each lead costs between £20 and £300, set by a scoring model that looks at loan size, revenue, time trading, credit history and affordability, meaning whether the amount asked for is realistic for the business's revenue.

In practice, RCF leads from established businesses with sensible asks sit towards the upper part of the range, because those are the enquiries lenders are most likely to fund. A long shot costs the minimum. Examples from the live model:

  • A £25k loan, £100k to £250k revenue, 1 to 2 years trading: 40 credits.
  • A £100k loan, £250k to £500k revenue, 2 to 5 years trading: 95 credits.
  • A £250k loan, £1m to £5m revenue, 5+ years trading: 165 credits.
  • A £5m ask from a business with £100k to £250k revenue and 6 to 12 months trading: 15 credits.

Loanleaf is a marketplace, so prices move with demand and lead quality and can change at any time without notice. You always see the price of a lead before you buy it, and if you use auto-buy you can set a maximum price per lead so it never spends more than you intend.

Setting up Loanleaf Platform for revolving credit work

Leads are sold on Loanleaf Platform, a self-serve portal. Before buying, you see a summary of every live lead: amount, purpose, timeline, revenue, trading time, credit history, existing finance and a quality score from 0 to 100. Contact details are masked, with checks for a valid UK mobile and a verified email domain. The business name and full contact details unlock when you buy.

You can buy one at a time, or switch on auto-buy so matching leads are bought the moment they arrive. Many brokers choose auto-buy for facilities, as speed to call matters. If several brokers match, a fair rotation decides.

A sensible starting setup might be:

  • Purposes: Cash flow, and Stock and inventory if you place them.
  • Time trading: 2 to 5 years and 5+ years, or lower if your lenders accept younger firms.
  • Revenue bands: £250k to £500k and above, adjusted to the facilities you can size.
  • Limits: leads per day, credits per day, credits per month and a maximum credits per lead.

Leads above your per-lead maximum are left for manual buying, and you get an email alert so you can decide for yourself. Brokers working in manual mode can also ask for an email when a matching lead arrives. Learn more about the mechanics on the auto-buy page.

Working an RCF lead on the first call

Speed matters because the owner has just asked for help and is expecting to hear from a broker. Bought leads are delivered instantly, on the platform, by email and to your CRM by webhook. The webhook sends JSON and works with Zapier, Make, HubSpot, Pipedrive and any CRM that accepts webhooks.

Open by showing you have read the enquiry, then use the call to fill in what the form leaves out. A short list is usually enough.

  • What the facility would be used for, and how often the business expects to draw and repay.
  • How the business is paid: invoice terms, card payments, direct debits or a mix.
  • What security, if any, the owner could offer, and who the directors are.
  • What existing finance is in place and whether it would be replaced or kept.

Loanleaf Platform includes a simple pipeline with the stages New, Contacted, Application, Funded and Lost, plus notes and CSV export. Keeping each lead moving through it is the easiest way to see which kinds of enquiry turn into facilities and which do not.

Revolving credit leads compared with other lead types

Not every cash flow enquiry is a facility enquiry. Term loans suit a defined purchase, merchant cash advances are repaid from card revenue, and invoice finance releases cash tied up in unpaid invoices. A revolving facility sits between them: flexible and sized against trading.

Because Loanleaf leads are not pre-sorted by product, the same enquiry might suit any of the four, which is why many brokers who write facilities also carry alternatives for the leads that do not fit.

If your business covers more than facilities, see the pages on business loan leads and commercial finance leads. You can read how credits and plans work on the pricing page.

Plans, refunds and getting started

Loanleaf works on monthly subscription plans, and prices exclude VAT. Starter is £2,500 a month for 2,500 credits, Growth is £5,000 a month for 5,000 credits, and Custom plans start from £6,000 in £1,000 steps. One credit is always £1, with no bonus credits, and plans over 20,000 credits a month are arranged with the team. Unused credits roll over while you are subscribed and expire when the subscription ends. You can cancel any time, upgrades take effect immediately and downgrades apply at renewal.

Refunds are available within 72 hours of purchase, but only if the phone number is not in service or belongs to someone else, or the email address does not exist. There are no refunds for any other reason, including a business deciding not to proceed.

To start, sign up at platform.loanleaf.co/register. You add your company details and lead criteria, choose a plan, accept the partner agreement online and pay by card, which takes about five minutes. Loanleaf checks each firm before it can buy leads, usually within one working day. If you would rather talk first, you can apply to become a partner and the team will get in touch. Questions

What are revolving credit facility leads?
They are enquiries from UK business owners who asked for finance on the Loanleaf website and whose answers suggest a facility could suit them, such as a cash flow purpose and an established trading record. Loanleaf does not run a separate facility form, so you judge fit from the lead summary and confirm it on the first call.
Are RCF leads exclusive?
Yes. Each lead is sold to one broker only. Once you buy it, it disappears for everyone else and is never resold. A repeat within 7 days comes back to you at no extra cost, and you never pay for the same lead twice.
Does the form tell me the business wants a revolving facility?
No. The owner chooses a purpose from a fixed list, such as Cash flow, Stock and inventory or Growth and expansion, but does not choose a product. Some enquiries will suit a facility and others will suit a term loan or invoice finance, so confirm what the owner actually needs on the call.
What does a business credit line lead cost?
Between £20 and £300 in credits, where 1 credit is £1. The price is set by loan size, revenue, time trading, credit history and affordability, and you see it before you buy. Prices can change without notice, so set a maximum price per lead if you use auto-buy.
Can I filter for established businesses only?
Yes. In auto-buy you can set criteria for loan size range, revenue bands, purposes, time trading and clean credit only. You can also browse the live leads manually and choose for yourself using the summary.
What does the form not ask about?
It does not ask about card takings, property value or loan to value, property address, planning status, sector, director names or bank statements. It also does not ask what security the business could offer. You qualify those points on the first call.
How quickly are bought leads delivered?
Instantly. A bought lead appears on the platform, arrives by email and is sent to your CRM by webhook as JSON. That works with Zapier, Make, HubSpot, Pipedrive and any CRM that accepts webhooks.
Can I get a refund on a lead that does not convert?
No. Refunds are only given within 72 hours of purchase if the phone number is not in service or belongs to someone else, or the email address does not exist. A business changing its mind or finding other finance is not a reason for a refund.
