Loanleaf Platform (platform.loanleaf.co) is the self-serve portal where UK commercial finance brokers and lenders buy exclusive business leads with credits, set up auto-buy, work leads through a pipeline and send them to their CRM. This page walks through each area: Dashboard, Opportunities, My leads, Auto-buy, Public profile, Billing and Account.

What the platform does

Loanleaf Platform is where everything happens once you are a partner. Leads appear there the moment they are created, you buy them with credits (1 credit = £1), and the full contact details unlock on purchase. Each lead is sold to one broker only, so a lead you buy never appears for anyone else.

You do not need a call with the team to use it. You sign up yourself, set your own criteria and decide whether to buy one lead at a time or let auto-buy do it for you. The sections below follow the menu in the order most brokers use it.

Getting set up

Registration takes about five minutes at platform.loanleaf.co/register. You are asked for these things in turn:

  • Your account login
  • Company details, including your company name, FCA reference where you have one, registered address, and the name, position and phone number of the person setting up
  • Your lead criteria, and whether you want auto or manual mode
  • Your public profile, which the business owner sees
  • A plan
  • The partner agreement, accepted online, with a PDF copy emailed to you
  • Card payment for your first credits

You cannot buy leads until Loanleaf has approved your firm, the agreement is signed and your account has credits. Approval is usually within one working day. You are responsible for your own regulatory position, including FCA authorisation where your activities require it. If you would rather talk first, you can apply to speak to the team.

Dashboard

The Dashboard is your starting point after you log in. Use it to see where you stand before you do anything else: your credit position and what has happened on your account recently.

A good habit is to open it at the start of the working day, check your balance covers the leads you expect to buy, and then move to Opportunities or My leads depending on whether you are buying or working.

Opportunities

Opportunities is the live marketplace of leads that have not been sold. Every card shows enough to decide without paying: loan amount, purpose, how soon the business needs it, revenue band, time trading, credit history answer, existing finance, a quality score from 0 to 100 and a price in credits.

Contact details are masked until you buy, but they carry two checks: whether the mobile is a valid UK mobile and whether the email domain is verified. A Companies House summary sits alongside. The full walkthrough is on the Opportunities page.

My leads

Everything you have bought lands in My leads, with full contact details and the business name. Move each lead through New, Contacted, Application, Funded and Lost, add notes as you go, and export to CSV when you need the data elsewhere.

If your team lives in a CRM, you can send each purchased lead there automatically by webhook, so My leads becomes a backup rather than the only place the lead exists. See lead management and CRM integrations.

Auto-buy

Auto-buy buys matching leads the moment they arrive. You choose the criteria (loan size range, revenue bands, purposes, time trading, clean credit only) and the limits (leads per day, credits per day, credits per month, maximum credits per lead).

If several brokers match the same lead, a fair rotation decides who gets it. A lead whose phone or email check failed is never auto-bought. Everything is covered on the auto-buy page.

Public profile, Billing and Account

Public profile: the logo, description and contact buttons the business owner sees in the email telling them they have been matched with you. It is worth getting right, because it is the business owner's first sight of your firm. See broker profile.

Billing: your plan, your credits and your payments. Upgrades take effect immediately and downgrades at renewal. Unused credits roll over while you stay subscribed. See credits and billing and pricing.

Account: your login and company details. Keep them current, because they are what Loanleaf and your own team rely on when something needs to be checked.

A sensible first week

  • Day one: finish onboarding and wait for approval
  • Before your first purchase: browse Opportunities in manual mode so you learn what the prices and scores look like
  • First purchases: buy a few leads that clearly fit, call them quickly, and log notes
  • End of week: look at your pipeline, then decide whether auto-buy would save you time

Delivery is covered in more detail on lead delivery, and the full onboarding route is on getting started.

Questions

Do I need to speak to Loanleaf before I can use the platform?
No. You can register yourself at platform.loanleaf.co/register. Loanleaf checks each firm before it can buy, usually within one working day. You can also apply to talk to the team first.
Can I see a lead before I pay for it?
Yes. Opportunities shows the loan amount, purpose, timeline, revenue, trading time, credit history, existing finance, quality score and price. Contact details are masked until you buy.
Is the platform only for auto-buy?
No. You can buy leads one at a time in manual mode, use auto-buy, or combine them. Auto-buy is a setting, not a requirement.
Where do my purchased leads go?
They appear in My leads straight away, are emailed to you, and can be sent to your CRM by webhook. Each lead is exclusive to you once bought.
Who can use the platform?
UK commercial finance brokers and lenders who want to buy leads. You are responsible for your own regulatory position, including FCA authorisation where your activities require it.
