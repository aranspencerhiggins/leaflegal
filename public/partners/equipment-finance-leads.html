What Loanleaf offers equipment finance brokers

Equipment finance is a specialist's business. The broker who knows a particular kind of machine, how it holds its value and which funders like it can place deals that a generalist would pass over. What that broker needs most is a steady supply of owners who have decided to buy and want to talk about funding.

Loanleaf is an introducer. Business owners find us through search, our own content and paid online advertising, then complete an enquiry form on our website. Those who pass the form are offered for sale to one broker. We do not buy in data, scrape lists or pass on cold contacts.

This page covers the leads that arrive under the purpose Equipment or vehicles: what they contain, what they leave out, how they are priced and how to set up Loanleaf Platform to receive them. If you work across a wider range of secured lending, our page on asset finance leads takes the broader view.

What an equipment finance lead contains

On the enquiry form, the owner picks a purpose from a fixed list. For equipment finance leads, that choice is Equipment or vehicles. It covers a wide range, from a CNC machine or a catering oven to a delivery van or a fleet of cars, and the form does not split it further.

Alongside the purpose, each lead includes the amount the owner wants to borrow, how soon they need it, the company structure (Ltd or LLP), annual revenue band, time trading band, any missed payments, defaults or CCJs in the last six years (No, Yes or Not sure) and whether the business has existing finance, with bands for the amount left to repay and the monthly repayments.

Contact details are the owner's name, business name, UK mobile and email. Each lead also carries the source page, a timestamp, the consent record and a quality score from 0 to 100 based on credit history, time trading, revenue, urgency and existing debt.

Where the leads come from and who qualifies

Every enquiry is filtered on the form before it is submitted. Businesses that do not meet the basics are turned away at that point, so they never reach the platform and you never pay for them.

  • UK limited companies and LLPs only. Sole traders, ordinary partnerships and businesses not yet trading are declined.
  • At least 6 months trading and annual revenue of £50,000 or more.
  • A loan amount chosen from £5,000 to £2m+.
  • A valid UK mobile number and an email domain that can receive email, both checked on the form.
  • No paying twice: a repeat enquiry within 7 days goes back to the broker who bought it at no extra cost, and after that you never receive the same lead again.

Before submitting, the owner confirms that they accept our terms and privacy policy, understand that Loanleaf is an introducer and not a lender or broker, and consent to their details being shared with a matched broker who may contact them by phone, email or text about business finance. The exact wording and a timestamp are recorded and supplied with the lead.

Machinery finance leads and vehicle finance leads

Because Equipment or vehicles is a single option, machinery finance leads and vehicle finance leads arrive together. Your first job on each is to sort them. Some brokers write both, many specialise in one, and a quick check of the amount and the owner's description usually tells you which pile a lead belongs in.

The amount helps. A request at the lower end of the range is often a single vehicle or a smaller item of kit. A request at the higher end is more likely to be a significant machine, a production line or a group of vehicles. That is a rule of thumb, not a certainty, and the call settles it.

The revenue band and time trading help too. A business with £500k to £1m revenue asking for £60,000 is asking for a modest share of its annual turnover. The same request from a company at £50k to £100k revenue is a different proposition. You see both on the summary before you spend a credit.

The honest limits of these leads

The Loanleaf form is short by design. For equipment, the useful details are the ones it does not ask for: what the item is, whether it is new or used, who the supplier is, whether the owner has a quote, how long the equipment would be used and whether it needs to be delivered by a particular date.

It also does not ask for sector, director names, bank statements, a property value or any detail about security beyond the finance already in place. The form does not ask about card takings either, which is not relevant here but is worth knowing if you handle more than one product.

All of that is first-call territory. A good opening is to confirm what they are buying and from whom, then ask whether they have a supplier quote. If they do not yet, the enquiry may be at an earlier stage than the urgency field suggests, and you can plan your follow-up accordingly.

How equipment finance leads are priced

Loanleaf prices every lead in credits, where 1 credit is £1 of lead value. Each lead costs between £20 and £300, set by a scoring model that looks at loan size, revenue, time trading, credit history and affordability, meaning whether the amount asked for is realistic for the business's revenue.

A sensible ask from an established business costs more, because lenders are more likely to fund it. A long shot costs the minimum. Examples from the live model:

  • A £25k loan, £100k to £250k revenue, 1 to 2 years trading: 40 credits.
  • A £100k loan, £250k to £500k revenue, 2 to 5 years trading: 95 credits.
  • A £250k loan, £1m to £5m revenue, 5+ years trading: 165 credits.
  • A £1m loan, £1m to £5m revenue, 2 to 5 years trading: 205 credits.
  • A £5m ask from a business with £100k to £250k revenue and 6 to 12 months trading: 15 credits.

Loanleaf is a marketplace, so prices move with demand and lead quality and can change at any time without notice. You always see the price before you buy, and in auto-buy you can set a maximum price per lead so it never spends more than you intend.

Setting up Loanleaf Platform for equipment enquiries

Leads are sold on Loanleaf Platform, a self-serve portal. Before buying, you see a summary of every live lead: amount, purpose, timeline, revenue, trading time, credit history, existing finance and a quality score. Contact details are masked, with checks for a valid UK mobile and a verified email domain. The business name and full contact details unlock when you buy.

You can buy leads one at a time, or switch on auto-buy so matching leads are bought the moment they arrive. If several brokers match, a fair rotation decides who gets it. A starting setup for an equipment broker might be:

  • Purposes: Equipment or vehicles. Add Refinance existing debt if you also refinance assets.
  • Loan size range: the tickets your funders will consider, for example from £10,000 up to the largest deal you can place.
  • Time trading and revenue: bands that match your panel, adjusted over time as you see what converts.
  • Credit: clean credit only, if your funders are cautious about adverse history.
  • Limits: leads per day, credits per day, credits per month and a maximum credits per lead.

Leads above your per-lead maximum are left for manual buying and you get an email alert. Manual-mode brokers can also ask for an email when a matching lead arrives. The auto-buy page explains each setting.

Working an equipment finance lead on the first call

Bought leads are delivered instantly, on the platform, by email and to your CRM by webhook as JSON. That works with Zapier, Make, HubSpot, Pipedrive and any CRM that accepts webhooks. A call in the first few minutes reaches the owner while the enquiry is fresh.

Use what the form has already told you, then fill the gaps with a short set of questions.

  • What is being bought, new or used, and from which supplier.
  • Whether there is a quote and the date the equipment is needed.
  • Whether the owner wants to own the asset at the end or simply use it.
  • How the existing finance on the lead is structured, and whether it could be replaced.

Loanleaf Platform includes a simple pipeline with the stages New, Contacted, Application, Funded and Lost, with notes and CSV export. Tracking the type of equipment against each lead shows you which categories you convert and which you should leave alone.

Plans, refunds and getting started

Loanleaf works on monthly subscription plans, and prices exclude VAT. Starter is £2,500 a month for 2,500 credits, Growth is £5,000 a month for 5,000 credits, and Custom plans start from £6,000 in £1,000 steps. One credit is always £1, with no bonus credits, and plans over 20,000 credits a month are arranged with the team. Unused credits roll over while you are subscribed and expire when the subscription ends. You can cancel any time, upgrades take effect immediately and downgrades apply at renewal.

Refunds are available within 72 hours of purchase, but only if the phone number is not in service or belongs to someone else, or the email address does not exist. There are no refunds for any other reason. See the pricing page for the detail.

To start, sign up at platform.loanleaf.co/register. You add your company details and lead criteria, choose a plan, accept the partner agreement online and pay by card, which takes about five minutes. Loanleaf checks each firm before it can buy leads, usually within one working day. If you would rather talk first, you can apply to become a partner. Our partners page covers the wider picture.

Questions

What are equipment finance leads?
They are enquiries from UK limited companies and LLPs whose owners chose Equipment or vehicles as the purpose of borrowing on the Loanleaf website. Each includes the amount, timeline, revenue, trading history, credit history and existing finance, plus contact details that unlock when you buy.
How is this different from your asset finance leads?
This page focuses on the Equipment or vehicles purpose and the first-call questions that go with it. The asset finance leads page takes the broader view across secured lending. Both draw from the same exclusive enquiries on Loanleaf Platform.
Can I tell whether it is machinery or a vehicle?
No. Equipment or vehicles is a single option on the form, and it does not ask what the item is or whether it is new or used. You find that out on the first call, along with whether the owner has a supplier quote.
Are the leads exclusive?
Yes. Each lead is sold to one broker only. Once you buy it, it disappears for everyone else and is never resold. If the same business enquires again within 7 days, the repeat goes back to the broker who bought it at no extra cost. After that, you never receive the same lead again and never pay for the same lead twice.
How much does an equipment finance lead cost?
Between £20 and £300 in credits, where 1 credit is £1. The price is set by loan size, revenue, time trading, credit history and affordability, and you see it before you buy. Prices can change without notice, so set a maximum price per lead if you use auto-buy.
Can I limit leads by loan size?
Yes. Owners choose an amount from £5,000 to £2m+, and in auto-buy you set the loan size range you want, alongside revenue bands, purposes, time trading and clean credit only. Leads above your per-lead maximum are left for manual buying.
How are leads delivered?
Instantly on purchase. A bought lead appears on the platform, arrives by email and is sent to your CRM by webhook as JSON, which works with Zapier, Make, HubSpot, Pipedrive and any CRM that accepts webhooks.
What if the contact details are wrong?
You can request a refund within 72 hours of purchase if the phone number is not in service or belongs to someone else, or the email address does not exist. No refunds are given for any other reason, including a business deciding not to proceed.
