Custom campaigns for lenders and brokers

A custom campaign is a dedicated lead generation programme that the Loanleaf team builds and runs for one lender or broker.

  • Exclusive: each lead sold to one broker only
  • UK limited companies and LLPs, £5,000 to £2m+
  • Delivered in real time to email, platform or CRM

A custom campaign is a dedicated lead generation programme that the Loanleaf team builds and runs for one lender or broker. It covers strategy, paid ads, creative, landing pages, optimisation and reporting, and every enquiry goes to you alone, in real time, with consent captured and checked in the same way as other Loanleaf leads.

What a custom campaign is

Most partners buy leads from the shared marketplace on Loanleaf Platform. Each lead is sold to one broker, but the leads come from Loanleaf's own marketing and are open to every approved firm. A custom campaign is different. It is a campaign designed around your appetite, run on your behalf by the Loanleaf team, and the enquiries it produces belong to you.

It suits lenders and finance brokers who know what they want to write and would rather have a team run the marketing than build one in house. If you only want to buy individual leads as they arrive, the marketplace described on the lead generation page is the simpler route.

What the Loanleaf team builds and runs

You are not handed a set of tools to operate. The team does the work, from the first plan to the monthly review. A custom campaign includes:

  • Strategy: who you want to hear from, which products you want to write and how the campaign should be structured to reach them
  • Paid ad campaigns: search advertising, Meta and other social platforms, and LinkedIn where it fits your audience
  • Ad creative and copy: the images, headlines and text for each ad, written to match what you offer
  • Landing pages and enquiry forms: pages built for the campaign, with forms that use the same checks as Loanleaf's own form
  • Management and optimisation: ongoing adjustment of budgets, audiences, ads and pages as the campaign runs
  • Reporting: regular updates on what is running and what it is producing

Which channels are used depends on your audience and your product. Not every campaign needs all three.

Targeting built around your appetite

A shared marketplace has to serve many firms at once. A custom campaign only has to serve you, so the targeting can follow your credit policy rather than an average of everyone's. Depending on what you want to write, a campaign can be shaped around:

  • loan types and the purposes you fund
  • loan sizes, from your smallest to your largest ticket
  • sectors you favour or avoid
  • regions of the UK you cover
  • annual revenue bands
  • time trading
  • credit profile

These are agreed with you before anything goes live and can be revisited as the campaign runs. If your appetite changes, for example a new product or a narrower credit box, the campaign can change with it.

What can be targeted in practice depends on the channel and the enquiry form. Anything the form does not ask, you would still qualify on the first call, as you would with any lead.

Where the enquiries go

Enquiries from your campaign go only to you. They are not listed on the marketplace and no other firm sees them. This is the main practical difference from buying leads: you are not competing with a rotation of other brokers for the same enquiry, because the campaign was built for you.

Delivery is in real time, through the same routes partners already use:

  • on Loanleaf Platform, where the enquiry appears for you to work
  • by email, as soon as it arrives
  • to your CRM by webhook, so it lands in your own pipeline without re-keying. The options are covered on the CRM integrations page

Consent works as it does for other Loanleaf leads. The enquirer ticks the boxes for the terms and privacy policy and for sharing their details, and the wording and timestamp are recorded and supplied with the lead.

The same checks as other Loanleaf leads

Landing pages and forms for a custom campaign are built on the same checks as Loanleaf's own enquiry form. That means the mobile number is checked as a valid UK mobile and the email domain is checked so that it can receive email, before the enquiry reaches you.

It also means the same honesty about limits. A form can only check what it can check. The first call is still where you confirm the details that matter to your credit decision. The lead verification page sets out what Loanleaf checks on its own leads and what is left for you to ask.

Loanleaf-branded or under your own brand

Many campaigns run as Loanleaf-branded campaigns. Loanleaf is the face of the advertising, the business owner enquires through a Loanleaf page, and the enquiry is passed to you as the matched lender or broker.

If you would prefer a campaign under your own brand, that can be discussed. It is agreed case by case. It also needs your own compliance sign-off of any promotion. Lenders and FCA-authorised brokers remain responsible for approving financial promotions made in their name, and Loanleaf cannot do that on your behalf. You are responsible for your own regulatory position.

How a campaign gets started

Every campaign follows the same sequence, with the detail set out for you at each stage:

  • Scoping call: we talk through what you want to write, the volumes you can handle and how you work leads today
  • Proposal: you receive a written proposal covering scope, channels, targeting, deliverables, terms and how the work will be reported
  • Build: the creative, landing pages, forms and tracking are produced, and delivery to Loanleaf Platform, email and your CRM is set up
  • Launch: the campaign goes live once you are happy with what has been built
  • Weekly optimisation: the team reviews and adjusts the campaign each week
  • Reporting and review: you get regular reporting and a review of how the campaign is doing and what to change

If you are new to Loanleaf, the how it works page shows how a standard enquiry travels from the form to your CRM, which is the same journey your campaign enquiries take.

Pricing and terms

Pricing and terms are agreed per campaign after the scoping call. There is no standard rate card, because scope, channels and targeting differ from one campaign to the next. Whatever is agreed is written into the proposal, so you know what you are committing to before the build starts.

Marketplace leads are priced in credits from £20 to £300 each and bought through a monthly plan. That is a separate arrangement and is not affected by a custom campaign.

Who a custom campaign suits

A custom campaign tends to suit firms with a defined appetite who want a steady, controllable flow of enquiries for it. For example, a lender focused on one product, a broker specialising in a type of asset or a sector, or a firm that wants to concentrate on certain regions.

It is also a fit if you have capacity to handle the enquiries quickly. Business owners who ask for a call expect one soon, and a campaign only works as well as the follow-up behind it. If you would rather start small and learn what you like, buying marketplace leads first is a sensible way to do so, and you can talk to us about a campaign when you are ready.

Questions

Who owns the leads from a custom campaign?
The enquiries from your campaign are delivered only to you and are not listed on the marketplace for other firms. Loanleaf is an introducer, and the consent record travels with each lead. The exact ownership and data terms are set out in the proposal and agreement for your campaign.
Can a campaign run under our own brand?
Possibly. Campaigns under your own brand are agreed case by case, and they need your own compliance sign-off of any promotion. Lenders and FCA-authorised brokers remain responsible for approving financial promotions made in their name.
How is a custom campaign priced?
Pricing and terms are agreed per campaign after a scoping call, and they are set out in the proposal before anything is built. We do not publish a rate card, because scope and channels vary.
How long does it take to launch?
It depends on the scope of the campaign. The timetable for the build and launch is set out in the proposal, so you know what to expect before you commit.
Is there a minimum commitment?
Minimum terms are agreed per campaign and written into the proposal. Ask about this on the scoping call, so that you can decide with the full picture in front of you.
How is this different from buying marketplace leads?
Marketplace leads come from Loanleaf's own marketing, and any approved firm can buy them, one lead to one broker. A custom campaign is built around your appetite, run for you by the team, and its enquiries go only to you. The marketplace is self-serve and priced in credits. A custom campaign is a managed service with terms agreed in advance.
