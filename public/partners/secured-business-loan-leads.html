What Loanleaf offers secured lending brokers

Secured lending depends on what stands behind the loan. A broker placing a secured business loan wants to know what the borrower owns, what it is worth and what is already charged against it. Those are specific questions, and they come after a business owner has first decided to ask for finance.

Loanleaf supplies that first step. Business owners find us through search, our own content and paid online advertising, including Meta, and complete an enquiry form on our website. We do not buy in data, scrape lists or pass on cold contacts. Every lead is someone who asked for finance, in their own words, on the day they enquire.

This page explains how to find secured business loan leads in the Loanleaf feed, what the leads do and do not tell you, and how brokers typically work them. We say plainly where the form stops, so you can decide whether it suits the way you qualify.

Where the leads come from and who qualifies

Every enquiry is filtered on the form before it is submitted. Businesses that do not meet the basics are turned away at that point, so they never reach the platform and you never pay for them.

  • UK limited companies and LLPs only. Sole traders, ordinary partnerships and businesses not yet trading are turned away.
  • At least 6 months trading and annual revenue of £50,000 or more.
  • A loan amount chosen from £5,000 to £2m+.
  • A valid UK mobile number and an email domain that can receive email, both checked on the form.
  • No paying twice: a repeat enquiry within 7 days goes back to the broker who bought it at no extra cost, and after that you never receive the same lead again.

Before submitting, the business owner ticks one box confirming they agree to our terms and privacy policy, understand that Loanleaf is an introducer, not a lender or broker, and understand their details will be shared with a broker we match them with, who may contact them by phone, email or text about business finance. The exact wording and the time they agreed are recorded and sent with the lead.

What the form does not ask about security

This is the most important point on the page. The Loanleaf form does not ask about property or assets. It does not ask for the value of any property, loan to value, the address of a property, whether a property is residential or commercial investment, or planning status. It also does not ask about sector, card takings, director names or bank statements.

So a lead is not a secured lead in the sense that the owner has described what they could offer as security. What you receive is a qualified enquiry for business finance, and your first call is where you find out whether there is an asset to lend against and whether the owner is happy to offer it.

Many brokers treat that as a feature rather than a flaw. The enquiry is not pre-sorted into one product, so you can offer a secured loan where it fits and something else where it does not.

Signals that point towards a secured loan

Although the form does not ask about assets, several answers can suggest a lead is worth a secured conversation. These are indicators, not confirmation.

  • Purpose: Commercial property (buy, build or refurbish). Someone buying or building property is the most obvious candidate for lending against it. Equipment or vehicles, and Refinance existing debt, can also be worth reading closely.
  • Loan amount. Larger asks, towards the upper end of the £5,000 to £2m+ range, are more likely to need security from the lender's point of view, though this depends entirely on your lenders.
  • Time trading and revenue. A longer track record (2 to 5 years or 5+ years) and a higher revenue band can support a larger facility.
  • Credit history. Each lead says whether there have been missed payments, defaults or CCJs in the last 6 years. Some secured lenders look past adverse history where security is strong, and some do not, so use the filter that matches your panel.
  • Existing finance. The lead shows whether the business already borrows, with bands for the amount left to repay and the monthly repayments. That points to existing commitments, and you can ask what is secured against what.

The quality score, from 0 to 100, combines credit history, time trading, revenue, urgency and existing debt. It is a sorting aid and not a view on security.

Working a secured lead on the first call

Because the form leaves security out, the first call carries more weight. A broker is not starting from nothing: the lead already shows the amount, purpose, timeline, revenue band, time trading, credit history and existing finance. Many brokers then cover the following.

  • Whether the business or its directors own property or other assets, and whether the owner would consider offering them.
  • The value of any asset, what is owed against it and who holds any charge.
  • For property purposes, whether it is residential or commercial investment, the address and the planning position.
  • Sector, directors and trading address, which the form does not collect.
  • Whether statements and supporting documents can be shared, and how quickly.

If there is nothing to lend against, or the owner would rather not offer security, the lead has not been wasted. You may be able to place it as an unsecured product, and our pages on commercial finance leads and asset finance leads show how the wider feed fits other products.

Setting up auto-buy for secured business loan leads

Loanleaf Platform lets you buy one lead at a time or switch on auto-buy, which purchases matching leads the moment they arrive. If several brokers match the same lead, a fair rotation decides who gets it. Each lead is sold to one broker only.

Because security is not a filter you can set, a secured broker usually narrows by the things the platform does offer:

  • Purposes: for instance Commercial property (buy, build or refurbish), Equipment or vehicles and Refinance existing debt.
  • Loan size range: the amounts your lenders work to, rather than the full range.
  • Revenue bands and time trading: the minimums your panel needs.
  • Clean credit only: on or off depending on your lenders.
  • Limits: leads per day, credits per day, credits per month and a maximum credits per lead.

Leads that match but cost more than your per-lead maximum are left for manual buying and you get an email alert. Manual-mode brokers can also receive an email when a matching lead arrives. The auto-buy page explains each setting.

What secured business loan leads cost

One credit is worth £1. Each lead costs between £20 and £300, set by a scoring model based on loan size, revenue, time trading, credit history and affordability. Affordability means whether the amount asked for is realistic for the business's revenue. You see the price before buying.

A sensible ask from an established business costs more, and a long shot costs the minimum. A £250k request from a business with £1m to £5m revenue and 5+ years trading is 165 credits, and a £1m request from a business with £1m to £5m revenue and 2 to 5 years trading is 205.

Monthly plans exclude VAT: Starter is £2,500 for 2,500 credits, Growth is £5,000 for 5,000 credits, and Custom starts from £6,000 in £1,000 steps. Unused credits roll over while you are subscribed, and you can cancel at any time. Prices can change without notice, so set a maximum price per lead. See pricing for more.

What you see before you buy and what you get after

Before buying you see a summary of each live lead with the amount, purpose, timeline, revenue, time trading, credit history, existing finance and quality score. Contact details are masked, for example a***@b****.co.uk and +447*******90, with checks for a valid UK mobile and a verified email domain.

On purchase, the business name and full contact details unlock. The lead is delivered instantly on the platform, by email and to your CRM by webhook (JSON), which works with Zapier, Make, HubSpot, Pipedrive and any CRM that accepts webhooks. The platform includes a simple pipeline with New, Contacted, Application, Funded and Lost stages, notes and CSV export.

If the phone number is not in service or belongs to someone else, or the email address does not exist, you can ask for a refund within 72 hours of purchase. Refunds are not given for any other reason.

Questions

Do these leads include details of property or assets?
No. The form does not ask about property or assets. It does not ask for property value, loan to value, the property address, whether it is residential or commercial investment, or planning status. You establish all of that on the first call.
How do I find leads likely to suit a secured loan?
Look at the purpose, loan amount, time trading, revenue and existing finance on each lead. Commercial property, Equipment or vehicles and Refinance existing debt are common starting points. These are indicators only, and you confirm security on the call.
Are secured business loan leads priced differently?
No. Every lead is priced between £20 and £300 in credits by loan size, revenue, time trading, credit history and affordability. The model does not price security, because the form does not collect it.
Can I filter by credit history?
Yes. Each lead shows whether there have been missed payments, defaults or CCJs in the last 6 years. You can switch on clean credit only in auto-buy, or leave it off if your lenders will consider adverse history.
Is each lead exclusive?
Yes. Each lead is sold to one broker only. Once bought, it disappears for everyone else and is never resold.
What if the owner has nothing to offer as security?
The lead is still valid. Loanleaf sells qualified enquiries, not guaranteed deals, and a lead stays valid if the business cannot offer security or is later declined. You may be able to place it as an unsecured or other product.
What happens if the contact details are wrong?
You can ask for a refund within 72 hours of purchase if the phone number is not in service or belongs to someone else, or the email address does not exist. Refunds are not given for any other reason.
Is there a minimum number of leads?
No. You subscribe to a monthly plan from £2,500 a month excluding VAT and spend your credits on the leads you choose. You can cancel at any time.
