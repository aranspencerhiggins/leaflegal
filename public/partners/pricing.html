How the pricing model works

There are two parts to what you pay: a monthly plan, and the price of each lead you choose to buy. The plan gives you credits every month. You spend those credits on leads, one at a time or through auto-buy.

One credit is worth £1 of lead value. There are no bonus credits and the value of a credit never varies, so a 2,500 credit plan is exactly £2,500 of lead spend. Plan prices exclude VAT, which is added at 20%, and you pay by card.

You never pay for a lead you have not seen. Every live lead on the platform shows its price in credits next to the amount, purpose, timeline, revenue, trading time, credit history, existing finance and quality score. You decide whether it is worth it before a single credit leaves your balance.

Monthly plans

Plans are subscriptions, billed monthly. Here is the full list.

  • Starter: £2,500 a month + VAT for 2,500 credits.
  • Growth: £5,000 a month + VAT for 5,000 credits.
  • Custom: from £6,000 a month + VAT, in £1,000 steps, with 1 credit for every £1.
  • Larger volumes: plans above 20,000 credits a month are arranged with the team rather than bought online.

Because a credit is always £1, moving up a plan does not change what a lead costs. It only changes how many you can buy each month. A firm that expects to take a handful of larger leads might sit on Starter, while a team working through a high volume of smaller leads might need Growth or a custom plan.

If you are unsure, start lower. Upgrades take effect immediately, so you can add credits the moment you run short. Downgrades apply at your next renewal.

The first month offer

New partners can take a first month offer: £1,000 + VAT for 1,000 credits. It is designed to let you try the platform with real leads before committing to a full plan.

After the first month, the plan moves to Starter at £2,500 + VAT a month for the following months, as set out when you sign up. You can cancel at any time, so the offer is a genuine trial of the leads and the workflow, not a lock-in.

A sensible way to use the first month is to buy leads in manual mode at first, so you see exactly how the summaries read and how the masked contact details unlock. Then decide whether auto-buy suits you. Our guide to auto-buy covers how to set it up safely.

How lead prices are set

Every lead costs between 20 and 300 credits. The price comes from a scoring model that looks at how fundable the enquiry is, not how cheap it was to generate.

The model considers five things: the size of the loan, the business's annual revenue, how long it has been trading, its credit history, and affordability. Affordability asks whether the amount requested is realistic for a business of that revenue.

This is why a sensible ask from an established company costs more than a long shot. A business with a few years of trading and a loan that fits its turnover is more likely to complete with a lender, so it is worth more to you. A business asking for many times its revenue after only a few months of trading is priced at or near the minimum.

Adverse credit answers (missed payments, defaults or CCJs in the last 6 years) lower the price, and an answer of "Not sure" lowers it slightly. You can read more on how this connects to the 0 to 100 score in our guide to the lead quality score.

Real examples of lead prices

These examples come from the live pricing model and show how the same factors move the price.

  • A £25,000 loan from a business with £100k to £250k revenue and 1 to 2 years trading: 40 credits.
  • A £250,000 loan from a business with £1m to £5m revenue and 5+ years trading: 165 credits.
  • A £5m ask from a business trading for 6 to 12 months with £100k to £250k revenue: 20 credits, the minimum.

Notice that the largest loan request in that list is the cheapest lead. The ask is far beyond what the business's revenue could support, so the model prices it as a long shot. That does not mean it is worthless, but you should go in knowing the realistic outcome may be a smaller facility.

Prices are set by the model and can change, so treat these as illustrations rather than a rate card. The price you see on the platform when you buy is the price you pay. To estimate what a typical lead would cost, use the cost per lead calculator.

The 72-hour discount on unsold leads

If a lead has not been bought after 72 hours, its price drops to 50% off. A 100 credit lead becomes 50 credits.

This matters for two reasons. First, if your criteria are narrow, you may see discounted leads that fall just outside your auto-buy settings but suit your appetite. Second, an older lead has had more time to go cold, so the discount reflects that you are calling later than an auto-buyer would.

Treat discounted leads the way you would any other: read the summary, check the timeline and credit answers, and call promptly. The consent record and contact checks are the same as on a fresh lead.

Refunds, rollover and cancelling

Refunds are deliberately narrow. Within 72 hours of purchase, you can request a refund on the platform only if:

  • the phone number is not in service;
  • the phone number belongs to someone else; or
  • the email address does not exist.

No refunds are given for any other reason, including a business that does not answer, has changed its mind, or does not qualify with your lenders. The checks on the form reduce bad contact details but cannot promise every owner will pick up.

Unused credits roll over from month to month while you stay subscribed, and expire when the subscription ends. You can cancel at any time. If you are ready to start, apply to become a partner or sign up directly on the platform. Loanleaf checks each firm before it can buy, usually within one working day, and you cannot buy leads until you are approved, have accepted the partner agreement and have credits.

Questions

Do prices include VAT?
No. Plan prices are shown excluding VAT, which is added at 20% and paid by card. The credits themselves are worth £1 each, so a 2,500 credit plan gives you £2,500 of lead value.
Can I see the price before I buy a lead?
Yes. Every live lead shows its price in credits, along with its key details and quality score, before you buy. Contact details are masked until purchase, and the business name and full details unlock when you buy.
What happens to unused credits?
They roll over from month to month while your subscription is active. When the subscription ends, any remaining credits expire, so it is worth spending them or downgrading at renewal if you consistently have a surplus.
Is there a minimum number of leads I must buy?
No. The only commitment is the plan itself, which you can cancel at any time. You choose which leads to spend credits on, whether that is a few expensive leads or many cheaper ones.
Can lead prices change?
Yes. Prices come from a scoring model and can change as it is updated. If you use auto-buy, set a maximum number of credits per lead so you never pay more than you intend.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately, so extra credits are available straight away. Downgrades apply at your next renewal, so you keep the benefit of what you have already paid for.
