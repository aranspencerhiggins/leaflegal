What auto-buy does

Speed matters with business finance enquiries. The owner has just filled in the form and is expecting a call. Auto-buy removes the delay between a lead arriving and you owning it. A matching lead is bought for you in the same moment, delivered to My leads and email, and sent to your CRM by webhook if you have one connected.

It is a setting, not a commitment. You can switch it on, change it, or go back to manual mode whenever you like.

Setting your criteria

Criteria decide which leads are eligible. You can set:

  • Loan size range
  • Revenue bands
  • Purposes, for example Cash flow or Equipment or vehicles
  • Time trading
  • Clean credit only, which excludes leads reporting missed payments, defaults or CCJs

Start with what you genuinely arrange. A broker who mainly places asset finance might choose Equipment or vehicles and a mid-range loan size. A working capital specialist might choose Cash flow with a minimum time trading. Broad criteria buy more leads; narrow ones buy fewer, better-fitting ones.

Limits that protect your credits

Limits stop auto-buy spending faster than you intend. You can set:

  • Leads per day
  • Credits per day
  • Credits per month
  • Maximum credits per lead

For example, with 2,500 credits on a Starter plan you might cap spend at 100 credits a day and 2,000 a month, leaving the rest for manual buys. The per-day lead cap protects your team's capacity: if two people can call properly about five new leads a day, there is no point buying ten.

Fair rotation between brokers

Leads are exclusive, so when more than one broker's criteria match, only one can get it. A fair rotation decides: the broker who auto-bought longest ago goes first. That means no single firm with the broadest criteria takes everything.

The practical effect is that you should not expect every match to be yours. Broader criteria and a higher maximum price per lead give you more chances, but rotation still applies.

Leads above your maximum price

If a lead matches your criteria but costs more than your maximum credits per lead, auto-buy leaves it. It stays in Opportunities for manual buying, and you get an email alert so you can look at it and decide. Higher-priced leads usually come from established businesses with realistic asks, so it is worth a look rather than ignoring the alert.

Why failed-check leads are never auto-bought

Every lead has its phone and email checked. If the phone number is not a valid UK mobile, or the email domain fails verification, auto-buy will not buy it, however well it matches. This protects your credits from leads that are likely to be unreachable.

You can still buy such a lead manually if you choose. Refunds are only for invalid contact details within 72 hours of purchase, so check the flags before you commit.

A worked example

Imagine an equipment finance broker on a Starter plan. They choose the purpose Equipment or vehicles, a loan range of £20,000 to £250,000, revenue of £250k and above, at least 2 years trading, and clean credit only. They set a maximum of 120 credits per lead, 3 leads a day, 150 credits a day and 2,000 credits a month.

A lead arrives asking for £60,000 for a van fleet, with £500k to £1m revenue, 5+ years trading and no adverse credit. It matches, costs less than 120 credits and passes both checks, so it is bought straight away. A second lead matches on everything but costs 165 credits. Auto-buy leaves it, and an email alert lets the broker decide whether to buy it by hand.

A third lead matches but its email domain failed verification, so it is skipped. After a few weeks the broker reviews My leads, sees which purchases turned into applications, and tightens or widens the criteria from there.

Setting it up well

  • Run manual mode for a few days first so you know typical prices and scores
  • Switch on auto-buy with narrow criteria and low limits
  • Check My leads daily and see which leads you are actually converting
  • Widen criteria or raise limits one change at a time
  • Connect your CRM, so a lead bought at 2am is waiting for your team in the morning

Questions

Can I use auto-buy and still buy manually?
Yes. Anything that auto-buy does not take, including leads above your maximum price, stays in Opportunities for you to buy by hand.
What happens when I hit a limit?
Auto-buy stops buying for that limit, whether it is leads or credits per day, or credits per month. Matching leads stay in Opportunities, and other brokers may buy them.
Does auto-buy guarantee a number of leads?
No. Volume depends on how many matching enquiries arrive and on the rotation between brokers. Limits only cap what you can spend, not what you will receive.
What if my credits run out?
Auto-buy cannot buy without credits. You get a low-credit warning email once your balance drops below 100 credits, so top up or upgrade before it matters.
Can I switch it off?
Yes, whenever you like, and go back to manual mode. Changes apply to leads arriving from then on.
Should I start with broad or narrow criteria?
Narrow, with low limits, then widen one setting at a time. That way you can see what each change does to the number of leads and to how many you convert.
