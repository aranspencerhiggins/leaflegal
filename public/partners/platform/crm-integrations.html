IntegrationsSend every lead to your CRM by webhook

Loanleaf Platform can send each lead you buy to your CRM the moment you buy it, using a JSON webhook.

  • Exclusive: each lead sold to one broker only
  • UK limited companies and LLPs, £5,000 to £2m+
  • Delivered in real time to email, platform or CRM

Loanleaf Platform can send each lead you buy to your CRM the moment you buy it, using a JSON webhook. You can add up to 5 webhooks, so one lead can reach several systems. It works with Zapier, Make, HubSpot, Pipedrive and any CRM that accepts webhooks.

How delivery works

Every purchased lead is delivered three ways: on the platform in My leads, by email, and by webhook to your CRM. The webhook is the one that saves time, because the lead is in your system before anyone has to copy anything across.

It applies to leads bought by auto-buy and to leads you buy manually. A lead auto-bought overnight can be sitting in your CRM, assigned and ready, when the team arrives.

What a webhook is

A webhook is a web address that your CRM, or a tool connected to it, listens on. When you buy a lead, Loanleaf Platform sends the lead's details to that address as JSON, a standard structured text format that most systems read natively.

You set it up in the Auto-buy area of the platform, where delivery by email and webhooks are managed. You can add up to 5 webhooks. A firm might send leads to the CRM, to a Slack alert tool and to a spreadsheet at the same time.

If you change CRM later, add the new webhook, check it works, then delete the old one. There is no need to pause buying while you switch.

What fields are sent

The webhook carries the same information you see in My leads after purchase:

  • Contact: name, business name, UK mobile and email address
  • Loan amount and purpose
  • How soon they need it
  • Company structure, revenue band and time trading
  • Credit history answer and existing finance details
  • Quality score, source page and timestamp, and the consent record

Because the payload is structured, you can map each field to a field in your CRM, for example loan amount to a deal value and purpose to a deal type.

Zapier and Make

If your CRM has no direct option for a webhook, Zapier and Make sit in the middle. Create a new automation that starts with "catch a webhook", copy the address it gives you, and paste it into the platform.

From there, you can build whatever follows: create a contact and a deal, assign it to the next person in a rota, post to your team chat, or add a row to a sheet. Test by buying a lead, or by checking the next one that arrives, and confirm the fields land in the right place.

HubSpot and Pipedrive

HubSpot and Pipedrive can both receive leads this way. The usual route is a workflow or automation that starts from a webhook trigger, or a connector tool such as Zapier or Make.

There is a HubSpot setup guide as a PDF at /partners/hubspot-guide.pdf. It takes you through connecting HubSpot step by step. Follow the same logic for Pipedrive: receive the lead, create the person, organisation and deal, and set the pipeline stage.

A worked example with Zapier or Make

Suppose you use a CRM with a deal pipeline and want each new lead to become a deal owned by whoever is next on a call rota. In Zapier or Make, the steps are short:

  • Trigger: catch the webhook from Loanleaf Platform
  • Step 1: create or update a contact using the name, mobile and email
  • Step 2: create a deal with the loan amount as its value, and the purpose and timeline as properties
  • Step 3: assign the deal to the next adviser and set a task to call within the hour
  • Step 4: post a short message to your team chat with the business name and amount

Add the webhook address in the platform, buy one lead, and check each field in the CRM. Fix any mapping that looks wrong before you switch on auto-buy, so the first overnight lead arrives properly formed.

Any other CRM

If your CRM can receive a webhook, you can connect it. That covers most modern systems and many older ones via an automation tool. If it can only take leads by email, use the email delivery and parse it with your CRM's inbox rules.

Not sure? Tell the team which system you use when you apply, or ask when you register.

Good practice

  • Test with a real lead before relying on it
  • Map the mobile number and email first, as they are what your team calls
  • Keep one webhook for the CRM and use others for alerts
  • Check that duplicates are handled the way you want
  • Remove webhooks you no longer use

Remember that your CRM copy is yours to look after, in line with UK data protection law. See lead delivery for the wider picture, and lead management for the pipeline view on the platform.

Questions

How many webhooks can I set up?
Up to 5. You can send the same lead to your CRM, a notification tool and a reporting sheet at once.
Do webhooks work for auto-bought leads?
Yes. Delivery by webhook applies to every lead you buy, whether auto-buy or manual.
Which CRMs are supported?
Any CRM that accepts a webhook, including HubSpot and Pipedrive, and any system connected through Zapier or Make.
Do I still get an email?
Yes. Bought leads are delivered on the platform, by email and by webhook, so you always have more than one copy.
Is there a guide for HubSpot?
Yes, a HubSpot setup guide is available as a PDF at /partners/hubspot-guide.pdf.
What if my CRM is down when a lead is bought?
The lead is still saved on the platform and emailed to you, so nothing is lost. You can copy it across from My leads or export it to CSV once your CRM is back.
