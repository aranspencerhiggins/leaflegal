BillingCredits, plans and invoices

You pay for leads on Loanleaf Platform with credits, where 1 credit equals £1. A monthly plan adds credits to your account, unused credits roll over while you stay subscribed, and each lead costs 20 to 300 credits. Upgrades apply immediately, downgrades at renewal, and you pay by card.

How credits work

A credit is worth £1 of lead value. Your plan adds credits to your account each month and every lead you buy deducts its price from your balance. There are no bonus credits, so 2,500 credits cost £2,500 plus VAT.

Each lead costs between 20 and 300 credits, set by a scoring model that looks at loan size, revenue, time trading, credit history and affordability. A £25k ask from a business with £100k to £250k revenue and 1 to 2 years trading is 40 credits. A £250k ask from a business with £1m to £5m revenue and 5+ years trading is 165. Leads still unsold after 72 hours drop to 50% off.

On a Starter plan you have 2,500 credits a month. Buying a 40-credit lead, a 95-credit lead and a 165-credit lead uses 300 credits, leaving 2,200. Because unused credits roll over, those 2,200 are still there next month, with the next 2,500 added on renewal.

On the first-month offer you start with 1,000 credits. That is enough for around 25 leads at 40 credits, or a handful of larger ones at 165, depending on what you choose. If you find you want to buy faster than the offer allows, an upgrade applies immediately.

If a lead costs more than you expected, remember that it is priced by fundability: a sensible ask from an established business costs more than a long shot, and that is usually the lead you want to be calling.

Plans

  • Starter: £2,500 a month for 2,500 credits
  • Growth: £5,000 a month for 5,000 credits
  • Custom: from £6,000 a month in £1,000 steps, with 1 credit per £1
  • Larger plans: above 20,000 credits a month, arranged with the team

All prices exclude VAT at 20%. New partners can take a first-month offer: £1,000 plus VAT for 1,000 credits, then Starter at £2,500 plus VAT a month. You can cancel any time. See pricing for the wider picture.

Pick the plan by the number of leads you can work well, not by the biggest number on offer. Most firms do better buying fewer leads and calling every one quickly than buying more than their team can follow up.

Roll-over

Unused credits roll over from one month to the next while your subscription is active. A quiet month does not waste what you did not spend, which matters because lead volume depends on how many businesses enquire.

Credits expire when your subscription ends, so spend them before you cancel.

Upgrades and downgrades

Upgrades take effect immediately. If you move from Starter to Growth, the new plan and its credits apply straight away, which helps if auto-buy is running through credits faster than expected.

Downgrades take effect at renewal, so you keep what you have paid for until the end of the current period. Change plans from Billing in the platform. If you need more than 20,000 credits a month, ask the team to arrange a larger plan.

The low-credit warning email

When your balance drops below 100 credits, Loanleaf sends you a warning email once. It is a prompt to upgrade or top up before you run short.

A balance under 100 credits can still be enough for several of the cheaper leads, but not for a 165-credit lead. If you run auto-buy, check your balance against your daily and monthly limits, as auto-buy cannot buy without credits.

Card payment and invoices

You pay for your plan by card, entered during onboarding as the last step. Your plan, credits and payments are in Billing, where you can also find your invoices.

For VAT, remember the plan price is quoted without it, so a Starter plan is £2,500 plus 20% VAT.

Refunds

You can ask for a refund within 72 hours of a purchase, but only when the contact details are invalid: the phone number is not in service, the number belongs to someone else, or the email address does not exist. There are no refunds for any other reason, such as the business changing its mind. Check the masked contact checks in Opportunities before you buy.

Budgeting well

Also decide how much of your balance you want ring-fenced for auto-buy and how much for manual buys. Leaving some credits for manual buying means you can still pick up a good lead that auto-buy skipped because it was above your maximum price.

Work backwards from deals. If your average funded deal pays you a fee, and about one in a certain number of leads converts for you, you can work out what you can afford to spend per lead. Your own pipeline data in My leads gives you the real figures. Start with the first-month offer or Starter, then upgrade as the numbers justify it. To begin, apply or register.

Questions

Do credits expire?
Not while your subscription is active. Unused credits roll over month to month, and expire when the subscription ends.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Cancel any time, and use up your credits first, as they expire when the subscription ends.
What happens if I run out of credits mid-month?
You cannot buy more leads until you add credits. You can upgrade your plan, and the upgrade applies immediately.
Why is there a first-month offer?
It lets new partners try the platform for £1,000 plus VAT for 1,000 credits before moving to Starter at £2,500 plus VAT a month.
Can lead prices change?
Yes. Lead prices follow a scoring model and the partner agreement allows prices to change, so check the price on each lead before you buy.
