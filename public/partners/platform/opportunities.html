Opportunities is the live list of unsold business finance leads on Loanleaf Platform. Each card shows the full qualification answers, a quality score, a Companies House summary and masked contact details with verification checks, so you can decide before you spend credits. Each lead is sold to one broker only.

What Opportunities is

Opportunities is the marketplace view of leads that no broker has bought yet. New leads appear as soon as they are created, and a lead disappears for everyone the moment someone buys it. Nobody else can buy it afterwards and it is never resold.

You are always buying a lead from a real business owner who completed the Loanleaf enquiry form and ticked a consent box to be contacted by a matched broker. The list gives you what you need to judge fit without calling anyone first.

What a lead card shows before you buy

Every card carries the answers the business gave on the form:

  • Loan amount, from £5,000 to £2m+
  • Purpose: Growth and expansion, Cash flow, Equipment or vehicles, Stock and inventory, Commercial property, Refinance existing debt, Tax or VAT bill, or Something else
  • How soon they need it: As soon as possible, Within 2 weeks, Within a month, or Just exploring options
  • Company structure: Ltd or LLP
  • Annual revenue band, from £50k to £100k up to £5m+
  • Time trading band, from 6 to 12 months up to 5+ years
  • Missed payments, defaults or CCJs in the last 6 years: No, Yes or Not sure
  • Existing finance, with the amount left to repay and monthly repayment bands where they have it
  • A quality score from 0 to 100, and the price in credits

The form does not ask about card takings, property value, sector or bank statements. Those are first-call questions for you.

Masked contacts and the two checks

Before purchase, the contact details are masked, for example a***@b****.co.uk for the email and +447*******90 for the mobile. The business name and full contact details unlock when you buy.

Next to the masked details you see two checks. The first confirms the number is a valid UK mobile. The second confirms the email domain is verified. A lead with a failed check is flagged, so you can leave it alone if you prefer. Failed-check leads are also never taken by auto-buy.

Companies House summary and quality score

Each lead also shows a Companies House summary for the business, so you can see at a glance that the company exists and matches what the owner told us.

The quality score runs from 0 to 100 and is based on credit history, time trading, revenue, urgency and existing debt. Treat it as a sorting aid, not a verdict. A 62 with a clear purpose and a short timeline can be a better call than a 78 that is just exploring options.

Filters

You can narrow the list the same way you would set auto-buy criteria: by loan size, revenue band, purpose, time trading and credit history. A common starting point is the loan range you actually arrange, plus clean credit if you work with mainstream lenders.

Use filters to speed up browsing, not to hide everything. If you filter too tightly, you may only see leads that auto-buy brokers have already taken. Loosening one filter at a time usually shows you what you are missing.

Reading the price

Each lead costs between 20 and 300 credits, with 1 credit equal to £1. The price reflects loan size, revenue, time trading, credit history and affordability. A realistic ask from an established business costs more. A long shot costs the minimum.

For example, a £25k loan from a business with £100k to £250k revenue and 1 to 2 years trading is 40 credits. A £250k loan with £1m to £5m revenue and 5+ years trading is 165 credits. Leads still unsold after 72 hours drop to 50% off. See pricing for the full picture.

Buying a lead

Open the lead, check the credits against your balance, and confirm the purchase. The credits are deducted, the lead is yours, and it moves to My leads. You also get the full details by email and, if you have set one up, by webhook to your CRM.

The business owner is then sent a "you've been matched" email showing your public profile, so call quickly. If the phone is not in service or belongs to someone else, or the email does not exist, you can ask for a refund within 72 hours. No other reasons qualify.

Questions

Can I see who the business is before I buy?
Not the name or full contact details. Those unlock on purchase. You do see every qualification answer, a Companies House summary, the quality score and masked contacts with their checks.
What do valid UK mobile and domain verified mean?
Valid UK mobile means the number checks out as a UK mobile. Domain verified means the email domain can receive email. Neither guarantees the person will pick up, but they filter out obvious dead ends.
Can two brokers buy the same lead?
No. A lead is sold to one broker only. Once bought it disappears from Opportunities for everyone else and is never resold.
Why does the same kind of lead cost different amounts?
The price is set by a scoring model that looks at loan size, revenue, time trading, credit history and whether the ask is realistic for the business. Two leads with the same amount can sit at different prices.
Will I be told when a matching lead arrives?
In manual mode you can get an email when a matching lead arrives. With auto-buy switched on, matching leads are bought for you instead. See auto-buy.
