# Loanleaf website: full specification for cloning into a UK law-firm lead generation brand

*Written 21 November 2026 from the live codebase. Secret values are never included, only variable names. "BP" means `tools/build_pages.py`. All paths are relative to the repo root.*

---

## 0. One-paragraph summary

Loanleaf (loanleaf.co) is a **free introduction service**. UK limited companies and LLPs fill in a multi-step enquiry form. Each enquiry is verified (phone, email, Companies House), priced in credits, and sold as an **exclusive lead to one specialist broker** through Loanleaf's own broker marketplace at platform.loanleaf.co. Loanleaf is a trading name of **Vertical Brands Ltd**. It is not a lender, broker or adviser and is **not FCA authorised**. Brokers pay (monthly credit plans through Stripe) and businesses never pay.

The site has two audiences, each with its own header, menu and theme:
- **Businesses:** light theme on deep green, about 210 SEO pages.
- **Partners:** a dark theme for brokers, about 42 pages, plus a law-firm partner page at /partners/legal.

| Layer | What it is |

| Domains | `loanleaf.co` (www redirects to it) and `platform.loanleaf.co`. DNS is at GoDaddy, with Google Workspace mail. |
| Database | **Supabase** Postgres, project `tefpljuzuvdchczcfjlz`, West EU. Row-level security is on for every table with no policies, so only the server key can read or write. Schema migrations are in `tools/platform-schema*.sql` (1 to 22). |
| Payments | **Stripe**: embedded checkout, subscriptions, customer portal, and a webhook at `/api/platform?action=stripe`. |
| Email | **Resend**. Transactional and CRM emails use branded HTML templates. |
| SMS / WhatsApp / lookups | **Bird**: phone and email lookup, SMS, and WhatsApp templates. |
| Company data | **Companies House** public API. |
| Calendar | **Google Calendar API** (OAuth refresh token) for partner call booking. |
| Bot protection | **Cloudflare Turnstile**. It is built in but **off** until both keys are set. |
| Tracking | Meta Pixel and Conversions API; LinkedIn Insight tag and Conversions API. There is **no Google Analytics or GTM**. |
| Live chat | Chatra, on partner and platform pages only. |
| Cron (vercel.json) | `/api/platform?action=cron-plans` daily at 07:00. `/api/crm?action=cron` daily at 08:00, weekdays only. |
| Tests | `node tools/test_flows.mjs` (126 end-to-end checks with outside services faked), `node tools/test_platform.mjs` (pricing and matching), `node tools/test_crm.mjs`, `tools/test_loan_math.cjs`, `tools/test_admin_finance.cjs`. |

**Deploy routine:** run the tests, commit, `git pull --rebase`, then `git push origin main`. Blog posts published through `/api/blog` commit to `main` themselves, which is why the pull before every push matters.

## 4. Lead capture flow (business enquiry)

### 4.1 Where it runs
- The root element is `<div class="ap-card" id="ap">` on `/`, `/apply` and `/business-finance`.
- `/business-finance` adds `data-variant="500k" data-amount="250000"`.
- Scripts load in this order: `cookies.js`, `track.js`, `apply.js`.
- `/apply` and `/business-finance` have their own locked design (`.ap-lp`). Sitewide styling changes deliberately exclude them.

### 4.2 Steps (`apply.js` `STEPS`, lines 46–91)

- Before step 14 a "finding options" loader shows once: about 900ms, or 300ms with reduced motion.

**Behaviour**
- One progress bar: width = (step + 1) / visible steps. Back button from step 2 onwards. Enter advances. Steps slide in and out (`apIn`/`apBack`).
- Answers persist in localStorage `ll-apply-v1` and are cleared on success.
- URL parameters:
  - `?purpose=<exact label>` and `?amount=N` preselect answers.
  - `?fbclid=` is stored as sessionStorage `ll-fbc`.
  - `?test=1` marks the submission as a test.

**Knock-outs**
- Screen heading "Sorry, we can't help with this one", with a "Back to home" button. Fires `FormDisqualified`.
- Sole trader or partnership: "We can only help limited companies and LLPs at the moment."
- Not trading yet: "We can only help businesses that are already trading."
- Not on Companies House: "We can only help limited companies and LLPs registered at Companies House."

**Validation**
- Name: at least 2 words.
- Email:
  - regex `^[^\s@]+@[^\s@.]+(\.[^\s@.]+)*\.[a-z]{2,}$`
  - typo suggestion ("Did you mean X?", Levenshtein ≤ 2 against 15 common domains, nudges once)
  - server MX check
- Mobile:
  - strip to digits; 0044/44 become 0
  - must match `^07\d{9}$`, then normalised to `+447…`
  - error: "Please enter a UK mobile number, e.g. 07700 900123"
- Server answers 400 `{field, error, suggest, confirm}`. The form shows the error inline and, where `confirm` is set, an "It's correct, continue" button that resubmits with `phoneConfirmed`/`emailConfirmed`.
- A company-name suffix check (Ltd/Limited/PLC/Cyf or LLP regexes) exists but is unreachable, because manual company entry was removed.

**Consent (version `2026-11-15`, `apply.js:371–373`)**
- Plain text under the button, with no checkbox. Clicking the button counts as consent:

  > By continuing, you agree to our Terms and Privacy Policy, and for us to share your details with a finance specialist who'll contact you about your options.

- The payload sends `consentTerms: true`, `consentShare: true`, `consentVersion`, `consentAt` and `consentText`. They are stored on the lead as `consent {at, version, text}`.
- Copy rules:
  - Consent wording must say "finance specialist".
  - Say "business loan", not "funding".
  - Don't state whether brokers are FCA authorised.
  - Use "pre-screened", never "pre-qualified".

**Payload (POST `/api/enquiry`)**
- Answers: amount (string), purpose, timeline, structure, trading, turnover, property, credit, debt, debtLeft, debtMonthly.
- Contact: fullName, firstName, lastName, business, email, phone.
- `company{number, status, type, incorporated, address, director, directorListed, role}`.
- Checks and consent: phoneConfirmed, emailConfirmed, the consent fields above.
- Tracking:
  - `marketing` (cookie choice), `fbc`, `fbp`, `eventId` (UUID)
  - `website` (honeypot), `_ms` (time on form)
  - `page`, `referrer`, `utm` (all query params), `first` (first-touch source), `variant`, `turnstile`

**Success screen**
- If matched instantly: "Thanks, {first}. We've matched you with {company}." Shows a broker card with Call / WhatsApp / Text / Email / Website buttons and a pre-filled message.
- If a repeat within 7 days: "You've already been matched with …", plus "Match me with a different broker" (signed link).
- Otherwise: "Thanks, {first}. You're all set."
- Always shows a summary and 3 next steps.

## 5. Where leads go after submission

### 5.1 `api/enquiry.js` pipeline (in order)
1. **Required fields:** POST only; firstName, lastName, business, consent flags; phone `^\+447\d{9}$`; email regex.
2. **Email domain:** MX check (falls back to an A record; a timeout passes). Error: "That email domain doesn't seem to accept mail. Please check it."
3. **Turnstile:** passes if not configured or if Cloudflare is unreachable.
4. **Company:** number must match `^[A-Z0-9]{8}$`. Then a Companies House profile lookup overwrites name, status, type, incorporated date and address, and adds SIC codes, industries, overdue filings, insolvency, charges and the director's birth month. The trading band is recalculated from the incorporation date.
5. **Meta flags:** lead = counts as a lead. "Qualified/premium" = revenue £500k+. Revenue tier events (C £100k+, B £250k+, A £500k+) fire only if trading for a year or more.
6. **Rate limit:** `enquiry:<ip>`, 8 per hour, otherwise 429.
7. **Silent drop:** honeypot filled, `_ms` under 5000, or `utm.test` → returns 200 but nothing is saved and no CAPI or Bird calls are made.
8. **Bird lookup (`verify.js`):** fails open, 4s timeout, one retry.
   - Phone: line type, carrier, reachable, allocated.
   - Email: result, reason, did_you_mean.
   - Hard errors:
     - invalid syntax or domain
     - undeliverable mailbox: "We couldn't find a mailbox…"
     - unallocated phone: "That number doesn't exist…"
   - Confirmable warnings: an email typo suggestion, or an unreachable phone ("We couldn't reach that number just now. Please check it's right.").
9. **Repeat check:** same phone or email within **7 days** (`REPEAT_DAYS`) → no Meta events. Otherwise Meta CAPI and LinkedIn CAPI events are sent.
10. **`ingestLead()`:** on failure, a fallback email goes to `ENQUIRY_TO`, subject "[Not saved to platform] Finance enquiry: {business} – {amount}". There is **no** admin email per lead otherwise.
11. **Response:** `{ok, match, again?, rematch?, meta:{lead, qualified, revenue}}`.

### 5.2 `ingestLead()` (`api/_lib/platform.js:741–820`)

**Insert:** one row in `leads` with:
- amount, purpose, timeline, structure, turnover, trading, credit, property, debt, debt_left, debt_monthly
- business, full_name, email (lower-case), phone
- consent, `source{page, referrer, utm, landing_variant, premium_tier, first, rematch_of}`
- checks, company, exclude_brokers, price

**Repeat within 7 days:** no new lead. `followUp()` sends, at most once a day:
- to the business: "You've already been matched with {company}", with a signed 7-day rematch link
- to the broker: "{business} has followed up: lead #{id}"

After 7 days, brokers who already bought that phone or email are in `exclude_brokers`, so they never receive or pay for it again.

**Price (credits, 1 credit = £1 + VAT)**
- price = min + (max − min) × size × affordability × age × revenue × credit, rounded to £5.
- Defaults: min 20, max 300, floor 40 when revenue is £100k or more, size from £50k (floor 0.4), adverse credit ×0.75, "not sure" ×1.
- Admin can change the weights (`settings.pricing`).

**Starter leads** (revenue under £50k, or trading under 6 months)
- Go free to a fixed starter broker (`STARTER_BROKER`) and are delivered immediately.
- Exception: under 6 months trading + homeowner + £250k+ revenue goes to the marketplace instead.

**Auto-buy**
- Eligible brokers:
  - approved, not suspended, mode = auto
  - agreement signed (or waived)
  - enough credits, criteria match, price within their maximum per lead
- Doubtful leads (phone unallocated or unreachable, email undeliverable) are **never** auto-bought.
- Rotation: `last_auto_at` ascending (fair rotation).
- Caps: monthly cap, tier budgets, leads per day, credits per day.
- The atomic SQL `buy_lead` decides the buyer; the first success wins → `deliver()`.

**No auto-buyer**
- Manual-mode brokers with matching criteria get "New lead: £X for {purpose} ({n} credits)" and can buy it on Opportunities.
- "Instant" brokers whose criteria don't match get "Outside your criteria: …".
- Invited, not-yet-signed-up firms get up to 3 "Matched in seconds" alerts a day.

**Discount and refunds**
- Unsold leads drop to 50% off after 72 hours (`DISCOUNT`, enforced in SQL). Not applied to £250k+ revenue or fixed-price leads.
- Refunds within 72 hours of purchase, for invalid contact details only:
  - phone not in service
  - phone belongs to someone else
  - email bounces

### 5.3 Delivery to the buyer (`deliver()`, `platform.js:628–648`)
1. **Email** to the broker's delivery addresses: "New lead: {business} – £{amount}", reply-to set to the lead's email.
2. **Platform:** "My leads" pipeline (New / Contacted / Application / Funded / Lost) with notes.
3. **Webhooks** (up to 5 URLs, https and public IPs only, 5s timeout, User-Agent `Loanleaf-Platform`):
   - Body: `{event: 'lead.purchased', lead: leadOut(l)}`.
   - Optional HMAC signing: headers `X-Loanleaf-Timestamp` and `X-Loanleaf-Signature: t=<ts>,v1=<hex HMAC-SHA256("<ts>.<raw body>")>`.
   - `leadOut` fields:
     - Lead and price: id, received_at, purchased_at, auto_bought, price_credits, tier, quality, companies_house
     - Business and contact: business, company_number, director_listed, contact_name, email, phone
     - Answers: amount, purpose, timeline, structure, annual_revenue, trading_for, property, adverse_credit, existing_finance, left_to_repay, monthly_repayments
     - Pipeline: stage, stage_at, funded_amount, archived_at
4. **Native CRM pushes:**
   - HubSpot: Forms API v3, portal + form GUID.
   - Pipedrive: API token; creates an organisation, person, deal and note.
5. **Customer told:**
   - Email "You've been matched with {company}" with the broker's public profile (logo, contact buttons).
   - WhatsApp template `loanleaf_matched_v2` (or SMS fallback) via Bird.

### 5.4 Partner applications and the admin CRM
- **`/api/partner`:**
  - Emails `ENQUIRY_TO`.
  - Sends Meta CAPI `SubmitApplication` (+ `PartnerInterest` for brokers).
  - Creates or updates a CRM contact (stage new or call_booked; tag "Law firm" for legal).
  - Brokers also get an invite email ("…you're invited to join Loanleaf as an approved supplier"), a WhatsApp/SMS welcome (`loanleaf_partner_welcome`), and an admin WhatsApp alert (`loanleaf_partner_alert`).
  - Returns an AES-256-GCM `handoff` token (30 days) that prefills platform sign-up.
- **Call booking:**
  - Google freeBusy over the next 14 days; 30-minute slots, weekdays 12:00–17:00 Europe/London, at least 2 hours' notice, excluding gov.uk bank holidays.
  - Creates the event "Loanleaf partner call (Google Meet|phone): {company}" with the applicant invited (`sendUpdates=all`).
- **Admin CRM (`/crm`):** a broker-acquisition CRM, not a lead CRM.
  - Stages: new, contacted, call_booked, proposal, onboarding, won, nurture, lost.
  - Stage flows send Resend emails and Bird SMS on a schedule (see section 6.9), at least 2 days apart, with one-click unsubscribe.
- **Broker onboarding steps:** account → company (name, FCA ref, address, name, position, phone) → leads (auto or manual, criteria) → public profile → plan → partner agreement (click to accept, PDF emailed) → Stripe payment.
- **Plans** (prices exclude VAT):
  - first month £1,000 for 1,000 credits
  - Starter £2,500
  - Growth £5,000
  - Custom £6k–£250k
  - invoiced plans set up by admin
- **Admin areas:** Finance (revenue, VAT, Stripe fees, Meta and LinkedIn ad spend, profit), Clients, Refunds, All leads, Pricing, Activity log.

### 10.2 What changes for a non-financial (legal) brand
